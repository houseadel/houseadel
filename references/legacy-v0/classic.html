Our Story

It began with rain, coffee, and nowhere to rush.

We met on a wet afternoon when the city moved slowly. One table was open, one umbrella was shared, and the conversation kept making room for one more question.

Amelia noticed Rowan first: linen shirt, coffee in hand, pretending to understand the book he had brought. Rowan remembers her laugh first. It was quick, bright, and completely impossible to forget.

Since then, our story has been made of Sunday markets, late dinners, balcony plants that almost survived, and the steady feeling of choosing each other in ordinary weather.

2019 First coffee, first walk home, first promise to meet again without pretending it was casual.
2022 A shared apartment, a tiny kitchen, and friends gathered close enough to become family.
2026 A proposal beneath frangipani trees, followed by a yes that felt like coming home.

Wedding Details

The rhythm of the day.

We wanted the day to feel like one long exhale: generous, candlelit, a little fragrant from the garden, and full of the people who helped us become ourselves.

4:00 PM

Arrive slowly.

Welcome drinks will be waiting near the lily pond. Find your seat, say hello to someone you have heard stories about, and let the garden do its quiet work.

4:30 PM

Vows beneath the palms.

The ceremony will be short, heartfelt, and outdoors. We will exchange vows in the lower garden, then steal a few minutes for family portraits while the first toasts are poured.

6:00 PM

Dinner at one long table.

Dinner will be served family-style with speeches between courses. Expect local flowers, soft linen, warm plates, and plenty of time to linger.

8:30 PM

Music after dark.

When the candles take over from the sun, the courtyard becomes the dance floor. Bring comfortable shoes and one song request you are not embarrassed to defend.

Dress code: garden formal, soft colors welcome. The lawn is real grass, so block heels or flats will be kinder to your evening.
