# Phase 1 research conclusions

**Status:** Evidence synthesis for creative-direction review; no positioning, governing concept, nexus, framework, renderer, CMS, or host is approved  
**Research date:** 2026-07-30  
**Evidence base:** the 22-reference landscape and matrix, pattern reports, market and service research, architecture comparison, three isolated prototypes, and the asset-pipeline study

## Executive conclusion

House Adel has a credible opportunity, but not as a generic “immersive studio” and not by competing with invitation platforms on features. The defensible offer is senior creative direction and production for **bounded, consequential moments**—occasions, releases, exhibitions, hospitality openings, and compact launches—where a specific visual world, careful integration, and reliable delivery matter more than application complexity.

The research supports **connected realities** as a strong internal governing logic. It does not yet support “multiverse” as public positioning, and it does not support shattered glass as the permanent interface. The logic scores **4.65/5** in the weighted evaluation when expressed subtly; the literal shattered-portal version scores **3.35/5** because it weakens client clarity, accessibility, production realism, and expansion.

The strongest initial experience architecture is likely a disciplined combination rather than one pure option:

- the small route scope of a compact commissioned-work portfolio;
- the clarity and resilience of an editorial work index;
- the future publishing potential of Editions; and
- one selective, skippable authored nexus or threshold.

That is a recommendation for review, not approval. The three prototypes exist to decide whether the authored opening should use graphic fracture, real-time glass, or cinematic/optical compositing.

## What the external evidence establishes

### Evidence

1. The strongest portfolios align a legible offer, an authored studio point of view, dependable project access, and a sustainable production burden. Continuous 3D is not the common cause of their quality.
2. Shopify Editions demonstrates a stable information contract beneath changing art direction. Studio Freight, Hello Monday, Locomotive, 14islands, Basement Studio, Obys, Garden Eight, and Dennis Snellenberg demonstrate that positioning, roles, and project evidence must survive the entrance effect.
3. Bruno Simon and Unseen show that a memorable world can coexist with direct HTML routes or explicit index links. Their work also exposes the danger of allowing the expressive layer and semantic layer to diverge.
4. Active Theory, Lusion, MakeMePulse, Dogstudio, Immersive Garden, North Kingdom, and Resn are ambition and production references, not realistic one-person staffing models.
5. Functional invitation, RSVP, release-routing, gallery, restaurant, booking, and campaign utilities are already inexpensive or productized. Bespoke value therefore sits in concept, art direction, asset direction, integration, stewardship, accessibility, and launch reliability.
6. Premium source assets are a gating dependency. A shader or transition cannot rescue generic, weak, or legally uncertain imagery.

### Inference

House Adel’s durable signature should be **editorial authorship**—how a reality is introduced, paced, connected, explained, credited, and delivered—rather than one recurring material, shader, layout, or client-facing aesthetic.

The practical model is a two-speed portfolio:

- direct semantic access for a visitor who already has a destination; and
- a bounded exploratory mode for a visitor willing to wander.

The index is not a fallback of lesser status. It is part of the authored system.

## Market conclusions

### What is credible now

- Invitations and private occasions are a credible proving ground and referral wedge, especially through planners, venues, and production partners.
- The object of sale must move upward from “a website” to an authored occasion, release, edition, or launch system.
- House Adel should integrate mature operational products for RSVP, ticketing, booking, pre-save, menus, and analytics instead of rebuilding them.
- A partner-shaped studio is more credible than pretending all 3D, film, sound, motion, and engineering skills are permanently in-house.
- International work requires visible operating discipline: English-first scopes, staged approvals and payments, named partners, revision limits, rights records, fallbacks, QA evidence, and clear support windows.

### Main commercial risks

- low-cost invitation and site platforms anchor buyers to commodity pricing;
- “focused digital experiences” is accurate but too vague without concrete buying moments;
- asset-heavy work makes estimation fragile;
- one-off launches create uneven revenue;
- AI-assisted production can create sameness and rights uncertainty;
- a spectacle-first portfolio can amplify perceived delivery risk instead of reducing it.

### Five positions kept open

| Position | Best reason to test it | Principal risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Private Occasion Atelier | Clearest entry wedge and strongest planner/venue channel | Price anchoring and being trapped in weddings |
| Cultural Editions Studio | Strong fit with editorial authorship and recurring frameworks | Smaller budgets, slow institutions, and a narrower buyer pool |
| Launch-World Studio | Strong budgets, repeat campaigns, and conceptual fit | Highest asset/specialist burden and game/metaverse language |
| Creative-Development Partner | Strongest repeat potential through agencies and design teams | Can subordinate Marshall’s art direction and overstate engineering depth |
| Focused Digital Experience Studio | Broadest long-term range | Weakest immediate category clarity |

No winner is selected. A plain descriptor such as **independent creative web studio** is the lowest-risk naming baseline while the offer is validated; more metaphorical labels should earn their clarity in buyer interviews.

## Multiverse conclusion

### Keep

- one authored studio capable of entering and constructing radically different project realities;
- a stable contract for route structure, credits, case evidence, accessibility, fallback behavior, and return paths;
- thresholds and transitions as meaningful connections between projects;
- an asset-first universe workflow with a world brief, visual bible, approved master frame, responsive exports, and provenance.

### Do not approve yet

- “multiverse” as the public headline;
- shattered glass as the permanent House Adel material;
- a one-screen nexus as the only project index;
- a persistent canvas;
- a fixed set of five worlds;
- lore, game controls, cosmic portals, or a theme-selector presentation.

### Strong alternatives

- an optical index using crop, refraction, and focus without literal breakage;
- a threshold-house grammar using apertures, curtains, rooms, and hosting;
- a recurring Editions cover above a stable work archive;
- an editorial index whose project routes own the radical art direction.

The governing idea and its interface expression must be approved separately.

## Experience and technology conclusions

### Canonical experience contract

Meaningful HTML, direct routes, browser history, focus, and project links remain canonical. WebGL may mirror and enrich that state; it never creates a parallel navigation system. Every route requires mobile, reduced-motion, no-WebGL, slow-network, and failed-asset behavior.

### Framework

Plain Vite + React + TypeScript is appropriate for the disposable Phase 1 lab. For a public build, React Router Framework Mode remains the leading architectural hypothesis because its documented pre-render plus SPA-fallback model fits a persistent React shell. It is **security-gated rather than selected**: the current dependency versions tested during setup produced high-severity npm audit findings and were removed. Next.js remains the escalation path if preview, ISR, or server needs become real; Astro becomes stronger if review selects a content-first experience with bounded interactive islands.

### Renderer

Use R3F as the leading hypothesis only if multiple React-owned project worlds and a persistent scene survive creative review. Use direct Three.js if the chosen idea is one compact compositor. Do not ship both principal architectures. Persistent WebGL must prove that spatial continuity adds meaning that DOM/SVG or cinematic compositing cannot provide.

### Motion

GSAP is the single complex timeline coordinator; CSS handles simple state transitions. Native scrolling remains the case-study baseline. Navigation must be cancellable, Back/Forward-safe, and immediately simplified by reduced-motion preference.

## Prototype interpretation

| Prototype | What it proves | Likely production posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — SVG/DOM fracture | Fracture can preserve real links, keyboard order, responsive reflow, and low runtime cost | Strongest baseline; refine only if the visual composition feels distinct enough |
| B — Hybrid WebGL glass | Real-time depth, raycasting, and a DOM-owned route can coexist with an embedded A fallback | Keep only if side-by-side review proves meaningful material presence worth the largest bundle, GPU, QA, and specialist cost |
| C — Cinematic compositing | Master frames and a small shader can create high perceived fidelity with little geometry | Strong contender if House Adel can sustain the recurring master-frame, matte, crop, and optional loop pipeline |

Prototype quality does not make its architecture final. The review should judge communication, atmosphere, ease of use, mobile reinterpretation, asset burden, and longevity before technical novelty.

## Asset workflow conclusion

The three fictional world frames validate the first half of the workflow: brief, directed generation, provenance record, master, responsive export, and static fallback. They do not validate a final House Adel aesthetic or production rights.

The recommended production gate is:

1. approve a written world brief;
2. approve one master frame before expanding the set;
3. record source, prompt/reference inputs, creator, rights status, dimensions, checksums, and transformations;
4. create desktop, tablet, mobile, reduced-motion, and failure exports;
5. measure weight and decode behavior before adding motion or 3D;
6. obtain human creative, rights, and technical approval independently.

Use generated or licensed assets as source material, not as an excuse to skip art direction. Advanced glass, complex character consistency, sound finishing, film, CGI, and campaign-rights review remain specialist-sensitive.

## Recommendations for the direction review

1. Shortlist three positioning options by buyer and referral channel, not by preferred visual language.
2. Approve or reject **connected realities** as the internal governing logic.
3. Compare the same three fictional projects in A, B, and C; decide whether fracture adds meaning or only spectacle.
4. Select one initial architecture among:
   - editorial/compact portfolio with a selective authored opening;
   - recurring Editions with a stable index;
   - nexus-led studio with an immediate conventional index.
5. Select the first proof world and fund its complete asset/fallback pipeline before multiplying universes.
6. Run buyer comprehension and destination-task tests before choosing public category language.

## Decisions reserved for the creative director

- first buyer and referral channel;
- public category descriptor;
- connected-realities logic;
- whether the word “multiverse” appears publicly;
- fracture, optical/cinematic, threshold, or editorial expression;
- initial route architecture and whether Editions launches now or later;
- first world and master frame;
- whether the visual gain of persistent WebGL justifies its burden;
- acceptable white-label/partner work;
- capabilities that require named specialists.

Until those decisions are recorded, Phase 2 production must not begin.
