# Multiverse evaluation

Status: strategic evaluation for direction review, not final approval  
Method: criteria derived from the House Adel brief; external reference evidence is linked through the landscape and pattern reports.

## The proposition

The strongest defensible version of the multiverse is not “many themes.” It is:

> One authored studio system that can enter, construct, and connect radically different visual realities for focused stories.

This describes House Adel's intended range without forcing client work into one house look. The concept becomes weak when “multiverse” is communicated literally through familiar entertainment imagery—cosmic portals, game maps, superhero fracture effects—or when every reality is the same page with a different texture.

## Criteria

Scores use 1 (weak) to 5 (strong). Weights reflect present business needs rather than visual novelty.

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Client clarity | 20% | Can a prospective client quickly understand what House Adel makes and why it matters? |
| Creative fit | 20% | Does the system express Marshall's range, art direction, storytelling, and curation? |
| Authorship | 15% | Can many aesthetics still feel like one studio rather than a marketplace? |
| Durability | 15% | Can the idea hold more work for three to five years without forced lore? |
| Production realism | 10% | Can an independent studio maintain it through an AI-assisted asset workflow? |
| Mobile and accessibility | 10% | Can the essential experience remain excellent without hover, WebGL, or long motion? |
| Expansion | 10% | Can another project or edition be added through a clear contract? |

## Governing-system comparison

Weighted totals are decision aids, not scientific measurements.

| System | Client clarity | Creative fit | Authorship | Durability | Production realism | Mobile / access | Expansion | Weighted total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Literal multiverse with shattered portals | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 4 | **3.35 / 5** |
| Subtle multiverse as connected realities | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **4.65 / 5** |
| The House: rooms, thresholds, and salons | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | **4.15 / 5** |
| Editions: a recurring publishing framework | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **4.65 / 5** |
| Portfolio index with changing project worlds | 5 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **4.55 / 5** |
| Constellation or atlas of worlds | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | **3.65 / 5** |

The scoring exposes an important distinction: the multiverse can be a strong **governing logic** while shattered glass remains only one possible **interface expression**. An Editions or editorial index structure can carry the same connected-realities thesis more clearly.

## Multiverse strengths

- It gives conceptual permission for radically different project art directions without implying inconsistency.
- It matches the studio's real value: directing a focused reality around a launch, occasion, or story.
- It can turn transitions and return-to-home behavior into authored moments rather than decoration.
- It supports future experiments and commissioned work through one universe contract.
- It gives AI-assisted asset production a useful role: each world begins with a deliberate bible and master frame instead of generic brand decoration.

## Multiverse weaknesses and risks

- The word carries entertainment-franchise, gaming, metaverse, and generative-theme associations that House Adel does not want.
- A client may remember the portals but not the service.
- Distinct worlds can look like template choices when titles, layouts, and interactions repeat mechanically.
- A one-screen nexus can hide project context, About, and Contact behind discovery friction.
- Strong portals require strong source assets. Weak portal imagery turns the system into an effects demo.
- Literal fracture effects are expensive to make tactile and convincing across WebGL, DOM fallback, touch input, reduced motion, and integrated graphics.
- A fixed set of shards can become a spatial CMS: every new project requires recomposing the entire hero.
- “Lore” can age faster than a quieter publishing or index system.

## Shattered glass as an expression

### What it communicates well

- simultaneous realities;
- one surface containing discontinuous images;
- rupture, refraction, and the moment of crossing;
- a memorable non-scroll interaction;
- material behavior that can connect DOM, SVG masks, layered media, and WebGL.

### What it communicates poorly

- care, intimacy, occasion, hospitality, and editorial intelligence;
- calm confidence;
- obvious project names, roles, and contact paths;
- easy expansion when shard positions are compositionally fixed.

### Failure modes

- impact-crack stock effect;
- superhero title sequence;
- selectable character or level map;
- five identical routes represented by five colors;
- glass material with no relationship to project story;
- desktop spectacle replaced by an impoverished mobile list;
- invisible links behind raycasting;
- transition timelines that trap Back, focus, or interrupted navigation.

### Conditions for keeping it

Keep shattered glass in consideration only if the prototypes prove all of the following:

1. Project names and purposes are legible before interaction.
2. Every portal is a real semantic link with direct-route and Back support.
3. The material feels editorial and photographic, not game-like.
4. Mobile is a deliberate reframed composition, not a smaller broken pane.
5. Reduced motion preserves the spatial idea without simulated camera travel.
6. A new universe can be added through data and asset contracts without rewriting the scene.
7. The first meaningful content and Contact path remain immediate.

## Alternative expressions of the same idea

### 1. Optical index

One composed field behaves like a lens, contact sheet, or light table. Focus moves between realities through refraction, crop, depth, and type—not literal breakage.

**Gain:** editorial clarity, quieter luxury, easier responsive reflow.  
**Loss:** less instant theatrical drama.  
**Prototype implication:** reuse portal imagery and shader compositing with a restrained optical mask.

### 2. Threshold house

House Adel is the stable architecture; doors, apertures, curtains, and rooms open into project worlds. The “house” is not a literal 3D building but a grammar of thresholds and hosting.

**Gain:** intimacy, hospitality, memory, and a natural About/Contact story.  
**Loss:** risks fashion-house or real-estate literalism.  
**Prototype implication:** transitions emphasize reveal and invitation instead of collision and fracture.

### 3. Living edition

The homepage is the current House Adel edition: one art-directed cover, selected works, and a stable index/archive. Every edition can radically change its art direction while the information architecture stays dependable.

**Gain:** durability, clear publishing cadence, strong return-visitor behavior, easy archive.  
**Loss:** less spatial continuity between simultaneous worlds.  
**Prototype implication:** Prototype C can become an edition cover rather than the universal nexus.

### 4. Curated constellation

Projects occupy a navigable relationship map. Proximity expresses shared subjects, scales, or media.

**Gain:** the connection between worlds is visible and expandable.  
**Loss:** abstract navigation, mobile density, and data-visualization/game associations.  
**Prototype implication:** better reserved for an archive view than the primary home.

## Mobile interpretation

Do not preserve desktop geometry at any cost. Preserve the governing relationship:

- show one active reality as a large tactile card or pane;
- allow short horizontal or controlled vertical movement between adjacent realities;
- expose an always-available “All worlds” semantic list;
- keep titles, project type, and status visible;
- use tap, swipe, and standard links; never require hover or device orientation;
- shorten entry/exit motion and avoid persistent high-DPR glass distortion;
- provide the same routes, not a reduced set.

## Accessibility interpretation

- DOM links own navigation; WebGL may mirror hover/focus and pointer coordinates.
- Focus order follows reading order, not shard coordinates.
- Current focus is visible through shape, type, and contrast.
- Screen-reader labels describe the destination, not “portal one.”
- Reduced motion uses crossfade, crop, and state change without simulated depth travel.
- No-WebGL mode retains the same composition through SVG/CSS masks or an editorial index.
- Essential studio/service/contact text remains outside the visual metaphor.

## Provisional recommendation for review

Retain **connected realities** as the leading strategic hypothesis because it fits House Adel's range and can become a durable universe contract. Do not yet approve “multiverse” as the public headline, and do not approve shattered glass as the permanent brand interface.

Use the three prototypes to answer a narrower direction question:

> Should House Adel express connected realities through fracture, through optical/cinematic compositing, or through a clearer editorial/edition system?

This recommendation remains reversible. The creative director can approve the governing logic while rejecting the word, the shattered material, or the nexus layout.
