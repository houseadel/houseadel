# Interactive portfolio landscape

Status: Phase 1 comparative research; not a final creative-direction decision.  
Access date: 2026-07-30 unless a source is explicitly described as historical.  
Evidence policy: direct sources are linked beside the claims they support. `Browser observation` describes behavior visible in the public site on the access date. `Inference` marks an interpretation that is not documented by the source.

## Executive finding

The strongest references are not the sites with the most continuous 3D. They are the systems that keep four things aligned:

1. a legible business proposition;
2. an authored studio point of view;
3. a dependable content and navigation contract;
4. an asset and technical burden the actual team can sustain.

For House Adel, the most useful composite benchmark is therefore not one site. It is:

- **Shopify Editions** for a stable information contract beneath radically changing art direction;
- **Hello Monday, Locomotive, 14islands, and Basement Studio** for cases that explain intent, roles, and production rather than only showing a hero;
- **Dennis Snellenberg, Garden Eight, Niccolò Miranda, and Obys** for credible small-team or independent positioning;
- **Studio Freight** for a studio-level operating idea that does not force every client project into one look;
- **Bruno Simon** as a narrow demonstration of how a single interactive metaphor, an open asset pipeline, and a separate HTML route can coexist;
- **Active Theory, Lusion, MakeMePulse, Dogstudio, Immersive Garden, North Kingdom, and Resn** as future-scale production references, not realistic Phase 1 operating models.

This comparison supports one cautious proposition: a “multiverse” can be defensible only if it means the editorial relationship between genuinely different client realities. It is not defensible as five visual skins, a portal selector, or a layer of shattered-glass effects. The current fragment nexus should remain a prototype hypothesis until it proves route clarity, interruption handling, mobile behavior, semantic reachability, and an affordable asset-production model.

## Method and limits

The comparison prioritizes official studio sites, official case studies, official repositories, and studio-authored process writing. Company-maintained LinkedIn and Behance profiles are used only where an official site does not expose company-scale context. Implementation is recorded only when a source names it. A visual resemblance to WebGL, Three.js, a CMS, or a framework is not evidence of that technology.

Three reference classes are intentionally separated:

- **Realistic peer:** an independent or compact studio/freelancer whose offer, team shape, or case-study model House Adel could credibly approach.
- **Future-scale reference:** a larger multidisciplinary studio or unusually high-production system useful for standards and ambition, but not a direct capacity comparison.
- **Narrow technical/framework reference:** a useful interaction, codebase, or publishing contract that should not be treated as a whole business model.

Mobile and accessibility comments are not conformance audits. They are either documented behavior or limited browser observations on the access date. “Unknown” means the available primary evidence did not justify a claim.

## What remains after the first 30 seconds

Hero spectacle is a weak test. The more useful question is what a potential client can understand after the entrance has passed.

| Pattern | References | Durable value | Failure mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A clear work index plus descriptive cases | [Locomotive work](https://locomotive.ca/en/work), [Hello Monday work](https://hellomonday.com/work), [14islands work](https://www.14islands.com/), [Basement Studio](https://basement.studio/) | A buyer can move from taste to proof: problem, approach, roles, deliverables, and result | A beautiful index without explanatory depth cannot demonstrate judgment |
| A studio operating idea | [Studio Freight self-case](https://studiofreight.com/work/studio-freight), [Obys about](https://obys.agency/about), [Garden Eight about](https://garden-eight.com/about/) | The identity persists while the client work changes | A repeated surface treatment turns diverse work into five versions of the studio itself |
| One literal interactive metaphor | [Bruno Simon](https://bruno-simon.com/), [Unseen](https://unseen.co/) | The interface becomes memorable and coherent | Destinations become hidden inside play, especially for keyboard, mobile, or time-poor visitors |
| A stable release grammar beneath changing worlds | [Shopify Editions](https://www.shopify.com/editions), [Spring ’26](https://www.shopify.com/editions/spring2026), [Winter ’25](https://www.shopify.com/editions/winter2025) | Audiences relearn very little while each edition earns its own art direction | The production model becomes a content factory beyond a compact studio’s capacity |
| Transparent specialist production | [Lusion about](https://lusion.co/about/), [MakeMePulse](https://www.makemepulse.com/who-we-are/), [Active Theory technology](https://medium.com/active-theory/the-story-of-technology-built-at-active-theory-5d17ae0e3fb4) | Ambition is matched by pipelines, tooling, and specialist roles | Copying the visual result without the pipeline creates slow, brittle, inaccessible work |
| Explicit individual offer and collaborators | [Dennis Snellenberg](https://dennissnellenberg.com/about), [Niccolò Miranda](https://www.niccolomiranda.com/), [Aristide Benoist](https://aristidebenoist.com/folio-v1) | A small practice looks credible because scope and authorship are understandable | Auteur styling can obscure client outcomes and repeatability |

For House Adel, the durable layer needs to answer four questions with no interaction trick required:

- What does the studio make?
- For whom and for which occasions?
- What role did House Adel play?
- Why was each project made this way?

If the nexus does not improve those answers, it is decoration rather than architecture.

## Comparative findings

### 1. The best studio systems distinguish house voice from client art direction

[Studio Freight](https://studiofreight.com/info) holds its work together with the operating promise “Moving Missions Forward,” while its [self-case](https://studiofreight.com/work/studio-freight) describes a flexible “schematic surrealism” illustration language for the studio rather than imposing one client style. [Obys](https://obys.agency/about) names typography, grid, motion, and concept as practice principles; its work still spans multiple client identities. [Garden Eight](https://garden-eight.com/about/) describes a seven-person studio united by craftsmanship and curiosity, not a mandatory visual skin. These are useful models because the invariant is a way of thinking and presenting, not a shader.

By contrast, a multiverse treated as a theme picker would make House Adel’s identity and client identity compete. The house layer should remain stable in voice, typography rules, credits, information hierarchy, and transition ethics, while project imagery, motion language, sound, and spatial behavior can change when the project idea demands it.

`Inference:` House Adel’s strongest potential invariant is editorial authorship—how a reality is introduced, paced, credited, and connected—rather than a recurring fragment material.

### 2. Central metaphors work when they clarify the relationship between actions and content

[Bruno Simon’s portfolio](https://bruno-simon.com/) is a literal drivable world. Its [official repository](https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025) documents a game loop, physics, compressed models, textures, and audio, making the metaphor a whole production system rather than an entrance animation. The result is memorable because driving is the consistent verb. Yet the public immersive page exposed only two unlabeled buttons in a browser accessibility-tree observation, while the separate [HTML version](https://bruno-simon.com/html/) exposes headings, project links, contact links, and lab entries. This is a valuable resilience pattern and also evidence that the expressive and semantic layers can drift apart.

[Unseen](https://unseen.co/) uses “click and hold” and drag-to-explore behavior inside a coherent studio world. On a 390 × 844 browser observation, it also exposed labeled `Enter` and `Enter without audio` choices, direct `Index`, `Projects`, `Contact`, and `World` links, and a sound toggle. That duality is more relevant to House Adel than the surface treatment: an authored world can coexist with direct routes.

[OFF+BRAND’s manifesto](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/off-brand) divided its argument into five gesture-led fragments involving taps, drags, and holds. The published description says it was not designed for conversion. That is valid for a manifesto experiment, but it is a warning for a portfolio that must establish services and trust.

The practical test for a House Adel metaphor is not “is it novel?” It is “does one verb help visitors understand how projects relate?” If visitors must decode the metaphor before reaching the work, the metaphor has reversed its job.

### 3. Persistent WebGL is not the default conclusion

The official evidence shows that specialist studios use real-time rendering where the concept or production brief requires it:

- [Active Theory](https://medium.com/active-theory/the-story-of-technology-built-at-active-theory-5d17ae0e3fb4) describes building Hydra after repeated high-demand interactive work exposed the limits of its prior stack.
- [Lusion](https://lusion.co/about/) explicitly offers WebGL, Unity/Unreal, installations, AR/VR, procedural modeling, asset optimization, and 3D pipeline work.
- [MakeMePulse](https://www.makemepulse.com/who-we-are/) documents a proprietary NanoGL real-time engine.
- [14islands’ GOALS case](https://www.14islands.com/work/goals-studio) explicitly names React, Next.js, WebGL, Three.js/React Three Fiber, Sass, and Sanity.
- [Bruno Simon’s repository](https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025) documents Three.js, WebGL/WebGPU pathways, Rapier physics, compressed glTF/KTX assets, and audio.

None of this supports WebGL as an always-on studio badge. It supports a single principal rendering architecture only after the project idea and asset pipeline justify it. The most reliable business-oriented references—[Dennis Snellenberg](https://dennissnellenberg.com/about), [Locomotive](https://locomotive.ca/en/work), and [Hello Monday](https://hellomonday.com/work)—make semantic project routes and cases do the core work even when individual projects use advanced rendering.

For House Adel, persistent WebGL should have to pass four gates:

1. the nexus requires spatial continuity rather than simply visual continuity;
2. the interaction remains understandable without hover, sound, or a custom cursor;
3. the team can generate and optimize all master and fallback assets;
4. route transitions, cleanup, loading, and low-power quality tiers are testable.

If any gate fails, a DOM-led architecture with isolated real-time scenes is the more honest prototype.

### 4. Navigation systems become stronger when the index is never sacrificed

[Studio Freight](https://studiofreight.com/) exposed direct Home, Work, Info, News, Aeon, and Contact links plus direct project links in a desktop browser observation. [Dennis Snellenberg](https://dennissnellenberg.com/work) maintains a current selection and a much larger archive. [Obys](https://obys.agency/works/) provides Vertical, Horizontal, and Grid viewing controls. [Shopify Spring ’26](https://www.shopify.com/editions/spring2026) uses stable named categories and in-page anchors even inside a highly authored edition.

These examples suggest a two-speed system:

- a direct semantic index for visitors with a destination;
- an authored exploratory mode for visitors willing to wander.

The index is not a fallback in the apologetic sense. It is the canonical route contract. Exploration is an additional reading mode.

`Browser observation:` On mobile, the [Obys home page](https://obys.agency/) exposed many direct links and view controls, but the accessibility snapshot contained very large duplicated link groups. `Inference:` the duplication was associated with multiple rendered layouts. This is not a conformance verdict. It is a warning that alternate views should not all remain exposed to assistive technology at once.

### 5. Native scrolling remains the baseline; scrolling machinery needs an experience-level reason

The official [Locomotive Scroll repository](https://github.com/locomotivemtl/locomotive-scroll) describes version 5 as a TypeScript library built on Lenis, using the native scrollbar and documenting keyboard and ARIA support. It also says touch parallax is disabled by default. That maturity is useful precisely because it makes constraints visible.

House Adel should not infer from Locomotive’s reputation that every route needs scroll interpolation. Project stories, credits, service information, and contact should keep native scroll unless a tested narrative depends on coordinated smoothing. Any smooth-scroll layer must preserve hash links, focus movement, browser history, Back/Forward behavior, interruption, and reduced motion.

### 6. Asset worlds are production systems, not moodboards

[Lusion’s service description](https://lusion.co/about/) explicitly includes procedural modeling, 3D asset creation and optimization, animation, and 3D pipeline work. [Dogstudio](https://dogstudio.co/studio/) lists brand, digital products, animation/illustration, interactive installations, and emerging technology. [OFF+BRAND](https://www.itsoffbrand.com/about-us) visibly names roles including 3D artist, WebGL engineer, technical lead, designers, and developers. [Basement Studio’s people page](https://basement.studio/people) calls out a 38-person crew connecting content and technology. These references can sustain radical project worlds because asset labor is part of their operating model.

Bruno Simon offers the clearest small-practice counterexample. The [official repository](https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025) documents Blender exports, glTF Transform processing, KTX textures, GPU-friendly ETC1S compression, WebP interface images, and runtime libraries. The visual world is not “made in CSS”; it has masters, transformations, exports, and runtime constraints.

For each House Adel project world, the minimum evidence package should therefore remain:

- brief and conceptual claim;
- visual bible and master frame;
- provenance and rights record;
- raw master separation;
- deterministic web exports;
- mobile alternative;
- static and no-WebGL fallback;
- load/error placeholder;
- owner and update cost.

`Inference:` A five-world launch created before this pipeline is proven would likely spend more effort simulating breadth than demonstrating the studio’s judgment. One or two thoroughly authored project realities would be stronger evidence.

### 7. Strong cases translate spectacle back into business evidence

[Hello Monday’s Google Cloud case](https://www.hellomonday.com/work/google-cloud-platform-website) identifies deliverables—development, UI, UX, concept, 3D modeling, and animation—and explains how four scales structure the narrative. Its [Lyft case](https://www.hellomonday.com/work/lyft) links a central flag metaphor to deeper driver stories. [Locomotive’s Elektra case](https://locomotive.ca/en/work/elektra-virtual-museum) explains an updateable museum platform and credits creative direction, art direction, frontend, backend, 3D/motion, project management, and account roles. [14islands’ GOALS case](https://www.14islands.com/work/goals-studio) connects the experience to recruiting and publishes the technology used. [Basement Studio’s Apollo case](https://basement.studio/post/launching-apollo-graphql-toward-future-growth) presents a launch in the context of future growth.

The reusable case-study sequence is:

1. occasion, client, and constraint;
2. governing idea;
3. House Adel’s role and collaborators;
4. selected artifact or scene;
5. interaction and narrative logic;
6. production and resilience notes where relevant;
7. outcome, status, or honest absence of measured impact;
8. direct next route.

This structure lets radically different work remain comparable without forcing it into a visual template.

### 8. Audio and motion need explicit alternatives

The observed [Unseen](https://unseen.co/) mobile entrance offered both audio and no-audio paths. [Bruno Simon’s site](https://bruno-simon.com/) exposes mute, quality, renderer, map, and respawn controls, while its [HTML route](https://bruno-simon.com/html/) provides a non-spatial content path. [Shopify Spring ’26](https://www.shopify.com/editions/spring2026) exposed headings, descriptive image alternatives, video controls, direct section links, and direct documentation links in the observed mobile accessibility tree.

Those patterns establish a minimum:

- sound must be opt-in or trivially disabled;
- motion controls must not be icon-only without names;
- direct links must not depend on spatial discovery;
- reduced-motion and low-quality modes should preserve narrative sequence, not merely stop animation;
- the no-WebGL route must be a first-class presentation of the same essential work.

Exact conformance remains unknown for most references. House Adel should treat their visible accommodations as patterns to test, not certifications to repeat.

## Implications for House Adel

### Recommended Phase 1 tests

These are research-backed tests, not approved creative decisions:

1. **Stable house contract test.** Build the same three project records in two radically different visual worlds while keeping title, occasion, role, credits, case sequence, direct URL, and return path stable.
2. **Nexus versus index test.** Give participants one destination task and one exploratory task. Compare a semantic project index, the fragment nexus, and one quieter relational model.
3. **Interruption test.** Interrupt every transition with Back, Forward, Escape, route change, tab change, and reduced-motion toggle.
4. **Asset-burden test.** Produce one world from source master through desktop, mobile, reduced-motion, no-WebGL, slow-network, and failed-asset outputs. Record labor and bundle weight before multiplying the system.
5. **Thirty-second test.** Ask a new visitor what House Adel makes, who it serves, and why one chosen project is distinctive after 30 seconds.
6. **Authorship test.** Remove all effects. If the studio’s point of view disappears with them, the governing system is not yet strong enough.

### Provisional architectural direction

The evidence favors a semantic route and content layer as the canonical architecture. An exploratory nexus may sit above or between those routes, but it should not own the only project links. One principal WebGL runtime could be justified if the approved nexus needs continuous spatial state; otherwise, isolated lazy-loaded scenes lower technical and asset risk.

Native scrolling should remain the default for cases. GSAP-style coordinated timelines and CSS state transitions are sufficient hypotheses for most DOM-led sequences; no source comparison justifies adding a second global scroll abstraction by default. This statement is an architecture recommendation derived from the evidence, not a claim about the references’ undocumented stacks.

### Creative-direction guardrails

- The multiverse is a relationship among authored realities, never five themes.
- The House Adel layer should stay identifiable without recycling a client aesthetic.
- Fragmentation must carry a specific editorial meaning or be retired.
- No portal, gaming-menu, glowing-button, particle-field, custom-cursor, or generic “AI dreamscape” convention should be used as proof of interactivity.
- Project depth and service clarity are part of the experience, not content added after the visual system.
- Every exceptional route needs an ordinary, direct, dignified route alongside it.
- Production provenance and fallbacks must be approved with the master asset, not after implementation.

## Open evidence gaps

- The exact current renderer, framework, CMS, and asset pipeline for most public portfolio homes are not documented. No inference should enter an architecture decision as fact.
- Current team size is imprecise for Resn, Aristide Benoist, Niccolò Miranda, Unseen, and Bürocratik. Their classification is based on public positioning and visible role structure, not a verified payroll count.
- Mobile behavior was directly observed only for Unseen, Obys, and Shopify Spring ’26 during this pass. Other mobile-quality entries in the matrix are deliberately conservative.
- Accessibility observations are limited snapshots, not audits. Keyboard order, focus restoration, reduced-motion media queries, contrast, screen-reader announcements, and route cleanup need House Adel’s own tests.
- Commercial outcomes are rarely quantified in portfolio cases. Where a source explains the brief or deliverables but not the result, the dossier does not invent performance.

## Conclusion

The landscape does not justify a final multiverse portfolio. It does justify a more exact prototype question:

> Can House Adel create one dependable editorial house in which different project realities remain radically themselves, while direct access, authorship, business meaning, and resilient delivery never change?

Shopify Editions proves that changing art direction can coexist with a stable content framework at extraordinary scale. Studio Freight, Hello Monday, Locomotive, Obys, Dennis Snellenberg, Garden Eight, and 14islands show more realistic ways to make positioning and project evidence persist after the hero. Bruno Simon, Unseen, Active Theory, Lusion, and MakeMePulse clarify the cost and opportunity of interactive worlds. The next phase should test the relationship among these lessons, not choose a reference to imitate.
