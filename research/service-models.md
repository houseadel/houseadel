# House Adel service-model research

**Status:** Phase 1 commercial hypotheses; not an approved service menu or rate card  
**Research date:** 2026-07-30  
**Source access date:** All web sources were accessed 2026-07-30.  
**Decision owner:** The creative director must approve the positioning and which models proceed to proposal testing.

## Evidence and estimate policy

- **Evidence** is accompanied by a direct source.
- **Inference** is a reasoned market conclusion not stated by a source.
- **Planning hypothesis** is a proposed scope, duration, or commercial term for testing.
- All House Adel price corridors below are **quote-test hypotheses**, not claims about average market price, not public launch prices, and not approval to publish a rate card.
- Specialist labor, licensed assets, travel, media, print, event hardware, translation, high-volume messaging, paid fonts, complex backend work, and taxes are excluded unless explicitly scoped.

Public pricing reveals a sharp divide. Productized wedding/release tools charge from free to hundreds of dollars or low millions of rupiah, while larger bespoke studios reviewed publish capabilities and inquiry forms but generally not fees. Rhumb Studio is a useful small-peer exception: it publishes fixed-fee entry points near €2,000 for landing-page sprints and €12,000 for full strategy/design/build projects. Sources: [Joy](https://withjoy.com/pricing/), [Riley & Grey](https://www.rileygrey.com/pricing), [Connectied](https://connectied.id/proposal.pdf), [Linkfire](https://www.linkfire.com/pricing), [Rhumb Studio](https://www.rhumb.co/), [Studio Freight](https://studiofreight.com/info), [Locomotive](https://locomotive.ca/en/agency), [OFF+BRAND](https://www.itsoffbrand.com/about-us).

## Governing commercial principle

House Adel should sell a **bounded authored outcome**, not hours of undifferentiated web production and not a list of effects.

Every commission needs five named layers:

1. **Idea:** what the moment means and what the experience lets the audience understand, feel, or do.
2. **Asset world:** imagery, typography, film, illustration, sound, 3D, and their rights/provenance.
3. **Experience:** content, hierarchy, interaction, pacing, responsive behavior, and operational destinations.
4. **Delivery:** development, integration, accessibility, performance, QA, analytics, hosting handoff, and fallback.
5. **Life cycle:** launch window, updates, archive/sunset, maintenance, and repeat edition.

If the buyer only needs RSVP, a pre-save link, a menu, or a viewing room, House Adel should recommend or integrate a mature platform. Paperless Post, Spotify, Linkfire, Artlogic, and Tock already specialize in those operational layers. Sources: [Paperless Post](https://www.paperlesspost.com/pricing), [Spotify Countdown Pages](https://artists.spotify.com/countdown-pages), [Linkfire](https://www.linkfire.com/pricing), [Artlogic](https://artlogic.net/), [Tock](https://www.squarespace.com/tock).

## Shared engagement architecture

### Stage 0 — Fit and constraint check

**Duration hypothesis:** 30–45 minute call plus a written go/no-go response.

Check:

- Is there one consequential moment?
- Is there one authorized decision maker?
- Are budget and launch date real?
- Do assets exist, or is an asset-production budget accepted?
- Which system owns RSVP, booking, ticketing, ecommerce, streaming, or records?
- What must be accessible, translated, measured, and maintained?
- Which rights, privacy, legal, brand, or institutional approvals apply?

No unpaid concepts, master frames, prototype interactions, or speculative production are included.

### Stage 1 — Paid direction and feasibility sprint

**Purpose:** reduce creative and technical uncertainty before promising the full build.

**Deliverables:**

- clarified brief and success behavior;
- audience and moment definition;
- two or three concept territories, not polished site designs;
- recommended territory with explicit tradeoffs, still subject to client approval;
- content/route outline;
- asset inventory, gap list, rights concerns, and partner needs;
- one master-frame direction;
- one interaction/motion test where necessary;
- delivery approach, integrations, fallbacks, schedule, and fixed production quote.

**Duration hypothesis:** 5–10 working days.

**Quote-test corridor:** USD 1,200–3,000, credited partly or fully to production only when stated in the proposal.

**Why it is separate:** Stink’s Chanel case and Studio Dumbar’s DEMO case both show that the valuable unit starts with strategic/creative concept and extends into several touchpoints; treating concept as free presales understates the work. Sources: [Chanel Chance](https://www.stinkstudios.com/work/chance), [DEMO 2019](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo).

### Stage 2 — Production

Production begins only after one direction, master frame, content owner, asset plan, scope, fee, and launch calendar are approved.

Suggested gates:

1. concept and experience architecture;
2. visual bible and master frame;
3. responsive key screens plus static fallback;
4. functional alpha with real content;
5. launch candidate;
6. acceptance and handover.

Every gate must identify the approver and the effect of late changes.

### Stage 3 — Launch and care

Baseline handoff:

- domain/DNS and hosting responsibility;
- client-owned platform accounts where practical;
- analytics and consent responsibility;
- CMS or content-update instructions;
- source and asset archive;
- license/provenance register;
- warranty window for defects;
- archive/sunset procedure for temporary campaigns.

Ongoing creative changes are not warranty defects.

## Service model A — Private Occasion World

### Exact job

Create a one-off digital and visual world for a wedding or significant private occasion, centered on a mobile guest experience and connected to the event’s wider identity.

### Target buyer

- Destination-wedding couple represented by a planner;
- private host with an event producer;
- luxury resort or venue offering signature occasion services;
- planner seeking a high-end white-label digital partner.

The Bali Wedding Association’s directory provides direct evidence of a professional ecosystem of planners, venues, photographers, decorators, caterers, and production suppliers. [Bali Wedding Association directory](https://baliweddingassociation.com/member-directory/)

### Core deliverables

- occasion premise and visual bible;
- typographic and image direction;
- responsive invitation/guest site;
- essential information, itinerary, travel, dress, map and contact;
- connection to client-owned RSVP/guest system;
- save-the-date and share-preview assets;
- two or three coordinated digital/print-ready pieces;
- reduced-motion, no-WebGL/static, slow-network, and failed-asset behavior;
- privacy/indexing choice and post-event archive or expiry.

### Optional extensions

- bilingual or multilingual copy/typesetting;
- custom illustration, photography, film, sound, monogram or lettering;
- printed stationery design;
- venue-screen loop or digital signage;
- guest segmentation and private itinerary views;
- post-event digital keepsake;
- on-site vendor coordination through a planner.

### Explicit boundaries

- House Adel should not become the guest-list database, payment processor, gift wallet, or on-site check-in operator unless a specialist platform/vendor owns that layer.
- No unlimited revisions.
- No unlicensed music or client media with uncertain rights.
- “Custom” does not mean every requested effect is added after approval.

### Duration and price structure

- **Duration hypothesis:** 4–7 weeks after content and hero assets are available.
- **Quote-test corridor:** USD 3,000–8,000 for the authored digital core.
- **Commercial structure:** fixed project fee; 50% booking, 30% at master-frame/experience approval, 20% before launch; third-party costs prepaid.
- **Rush hypothesis:** only offered after asset review, with reduced scope and a 25–50% capacity premium.

These amounts intentionally sit outside the template comparison. For context, local template offers list Rp79,000–Rp499,000, while Connectied lists custom design and illustration as Rp2.1m and Rp5.25m add-ons. Sources: [Wedera](https://www.wedera.id/), [Wedly](https://wedly.id/), [Connectied](https://connectied.id/proposal.pdf). The House Adel corridor is a positioning test, not evidence of established local willingness to pay.

### Repeat potential

- **Direct couple:** low.
- **Planner, venue, or hospitality partner:** high if the process is reliable and each world remains distinct.
- **Extensions:** medium through anniversaries, private dinners, post-event archive, or family occasions.

### Partners

Planner/producer, stationer, calligrapher/type designer, photographer/filmmaker, illustrator, translator, RSVP/check-in vendor, print producer.

### Proof required before selling broadly

One complete specimen or real case that shows the brief, world bible, mobile guest flow, operational clarity, paper/digital relationship, credits, fallbacks, and asset provenance. A beautiful desktop hero is insufficient.

## Service model B — Event Identity and Digital Guest Journey

### Exact job

Create a flexible identity system and operational website for a festival, conference, award, exhibition opening, or recurring public event.

### Target buyer

Independent festivals, cultural programs, creative conferences, awards, brand events, hospitality series, and event producers.

### Core deliverables

- event premise, visual/verbal direction and identity rules;
- motion grammar and templates;
- responsive site with program, participants, venue, travel/access, partners and contact;
- ticketing/registration integration;
- announcement, ticket-live, program-live, sold-out, live-event and archive states;
- social launch kit and email header system;
- stage/screen or signage rules where commissioned;
- CMS/content model for schedules and participants;
- accessibility, performance and launch QA;
- edition/archive strategy.

Studio Dumbar’s DEMO case supports this cross-touchpoint model: its identity, website, trailers, custom type behavior, and station screens operate as one system. [DEMO 2019](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo)

### Duration and price structure

- **Duration hypothesis:** 7–12 weeks for a first edition; 3–6 weeks for later editions using an approved system.
- **Quote-test corridor:** USD 7,000–18,000 for identity plus a bounded site; extensive signage, production art, motion packages, or custom content priced separately.
- **Commercial structure:** fixed first-edition fee, then an annual edition fee or monthly launch-window retainer.

### Repeat potential

High. An annual event can buy:

- edition concept and identity evolution;
- speaker/artist waves;
- schedule/content updates;
- sponsor and venue assets;
- launch support;
- archive and next-edition reset.

The system must permit change without turning each edition into a skin.

### Partners

Event producer, ticketing provider, venue/AV supplier, motion designer, copywriter, signage/print producer, photographer/film team, accessibility reviewer.

### Main risk

Operational content changes late. The proposal needs content templates, a data owner, freeze dates, emergency-update rules, and a conventional information route even if the expressive layer is experimental.

## Service model C — Release or Exhibition Edition

### Exact job

Build an authored digital edition around an album, tour, artist chapter, exhibition, publication, archive, or cultural release.

### Target buyer

Artist management, independent label, gallery, curator, foundation, publisher, fashion/culture collaborator, university program, or brand sponsoring culture.

### Core deliverables

- release/exhibition premise and editorial structure;
- world bible and asset-direction requirements;
- responsive experience with story, works/tracks, participants, credits and destinations;
- Linkfire/Spotify/Music Glue/ticketing/store or Artlogic/CMS integration;
- one meaningful participatory, spatial, or editorial mechanic where justified;
- press/share assets;
- accessibility and media alternatives;
- analytics events tied to intended action;
- archive behavior after the campaign/exhibition.

### Product boundary

House Adel does not replace:

- streaming service and pre-save infrastructure;
- ecommerce, fulfillment, ticketing or CRM;
- gallery inventory and sales records;
- curatorial authority, rights clearance, or scholarly fact checking.

Spotify, Linkfire, Music Glue, and Artlogic provide these mature layers. Sources: [Spotify Countdown Pages](https://artists.spotify.com/countdown-pages), [Linkfire](https://www.linkfire.com/pricing), [Music Glue](https://www.musicglue.com/), [Artlogic](https://artlogic.net/).

### Duration and price structure

- **Duration hypothesis:** 4–9 weeks for one edition; longer where curatorial content, rights or original production are substantial.
- **Quote-test corridor:** USD 4,500–14,000.
- **Commercial structure:** fixed edition fee; label/management/gallery relationships can add a roster or season retainer with separately reserved production slots.

### Repeat potential

- **Artist alone:** medium and budget-dependent.
- **Manager/label:** high across singles, albums, tours, merch, anniversaries and roster releases.
- **Gallery/foundation:** high across exhibitions and art-fair moments if the platform is reusable.

### Partners

Label/manager, campaign manager, curator/editor, photographer/director, motion/sound designer, type designer, 3D specialist, Linkfire/Music Glue/Artlogic implementer, rights administrator.

### Main risk

Audience action is easily lost inside spectacle. Every concept must preserve immediate paths to listen, buy, attend, read, or view. UNIT9’s Coke Studio work is a future-scale reference for artist-specific interactive worlds, not permission to add a game to every release. [Coke Studio Virtual Record Store](https://www.unit9.com/project/coke-studio-real-magic-virtual-record-store)

## Service model D — Hospitality or Product Launch Chapter

### Exact job

Create the focused launch experience for a new restaurant, boutique property, fragrance, beauty product, object, or limited collection while connecting to booking, retail, or the permanent brand site.

### Target buyer

- Chef/founder opening a well-funded concept;
- boutique hotel, villa, members’ space, or hospitality group;
- independent fragrance, beauty, fashion, jewelry, food/drink, or design-object brand;
- distributor launching a product in a region;
- brand or PR agency needing an authored digital chapter.

### Core deliverables

- product/place premise and narrative sequence;
- asset brief, world bible and master frame;
- pre-launch/teaser and live states;
- responsive launch destination;
- photography/film/CGI integration;
- booking, retailer, ecommerce, map, menu or stockist paths;
- share and press assets;
- analytics and campaign tags;
- mobile/reduced-motion/static/failure behavior;
- archive, redirect or permanent handoff.

### Optional extensions

- photography/film creative direction;
- social motion loops and story crops;
- retail/display loop;
- localization;
- influencer or press-specific route;
- product visualization;
- launch-day content and post-launch optimization.

### Duration and price structure

- **Duration hypothesis:** 6–12 weeks; the schedule begins only when product/place access and asset production are credible.
- **Quote-test corridor:** USD 8,000–24,000 for a compact launch chapter.
- **Commercial structure:** fixed fee plus asset/specialist pass-through; a PR/brand partner may prefer a burst retainer reserving capacity from teaser through post-launch.

### Repeat potential

High for groups and agencies with seasonal menus, new locations, product drops, collections, and market localization. Lower for a single venue with no ongoing campaign plan.

### Evidence and scale caution

Squarespace, Wix, BentoBox, and Tock already solve standard restaurant presence and operations. Sources: [Squarespace Restaurants](https://www.squarespace.com/tour/restaurant-websites), [Wix Restaurants](https://www.wix.com/restaurant/website), [BentoBox](https://www.getbento.com/features/restaurant-website-design/), [Tock](https://www.squarespace.com/tock). Stink’s Chanel work shows that a global fragrance commission can extend across strategy, interactive film, mobile/tablet, localization, advertising and regional platforms. [Chanel Chance](https://www.stinkstudios.com/work/chance) The House Adel version must be intentionally smaller and partner-supported.

### Main risk

Weak or late imagery. This model requires an asset-production gate. No amount of shader, scroll motion, or typography can substitute for inaccurate product imagery or generic location photography.

## Service model E — Compact Campaign Experience

### Exact job

Give one campaign, anniversary, cause, report, collaboration, or brand moment a distinct digital container when the main site is the wrong format.

RIOT’s public definition is useful: a microsite is a focused experience around a single idea, campaign, launch, or moment, smaller than a full website but concentrated in tone, narrative, and purpose. [RIOT microsites](https://riot.nyc/services/digital/microsites/)

### Target buyer

Brand team, funded startup, entertainment property, publisher, nonprofit with campaign funding, PR/advertising agency, production company, or brand studio.

### Core deliverables

- brief reframing and one governing idea;
- audience action and measurement plan;
- master frame and asset plan;
- responsive microsite/experience;
- one central interaction or narrative behavior;
- main-site/CRM/ecommerce/form integration;
- campaign metadata, analytics and consent implementation;
- launch, QA and failure plan;
- archive/sunset.

### Duration and price structure

- **Duration hypothesis:** 6–12 weeks.
- **Quote-test corridor:** USD 10,000–30,000.
- **Commercial structure:** fixed fee for stable scope; paid discovery when idea/assets are unresolved; burst retainer where campaigns overlap; change orders for approved scope movement.

### Repeat potential

High with an agency or in-house team; low with a one-time founder lacking a campaign calendar.

### Main risk

Calling an ordinary landing page a “digital experience.” The project should only exist separately when its idea, audience, data boundary, campaign window, or content structure genuinely needs a separate container.

## Service model F — Creative-Development Partner

### Exact job

Join a design, brand, event, PR, or production team as the senior digital concept and implementation partner for a bounded experience.

### Target buyer

Studios and agencies that have client access, strategy, identity, campaign management, or assets but lack creative-development capacity.

### Possible engagement modes

1. **Build partner:** supplied approved design; House Adel handles interaction, responsive interpretation, development, accessibility, performance and launch.
2. **Concept + build partner:** House Adel joins before design freeze to shape the digital idea and feasibility.
3. **Prototype/R&D partner:** a short paid experiment establishes interaction, asset method, or technical risk.
4. **Reserved-capacity retainer:** a partner books a defined number of days or one small launch slot per month.

### Deliverables and boundaries

- role/credit and portfolio rights agreed before work;
- one named partner-side producer and approver;
- design-source and asset handoff standards;
- browser/device matrix and acceptance criteria;
- clear responsibility for client communication, data, copy, rights, hosting and support;
- no invisible assumption that House Adel provides unbudgeted 3D, motion, QA, or back-end work.

### Duration and price structure

- **Duration hypothesis:** 2–10 weeks depending on role.
- **Quote-test corridor:** USD 3,000–18,000 per bounded build; R&D sprint USD 1,200–3,000; reserved capacity quoted by actual days and response window.
- **Commercial structure:** fixed milestone fee for a stable deliverable; day blocks for genuinely variable embedded work; monthly retainer only with minimum term, capacity ceiling and rollover rules.

### Repeat potential

Very high if delivery is dependable. It is also the clearest path to commercial campaigns without pretending House Adel already has direct global-brand relationships.

### Weakness

White-label work can leave no public evidence. Negotiate credited, delayed, anonymized, or private case-study rights. Maintain a balance between partner revenue and direct authored work.

## Commercial structures to use selectively

| Structure | Best fit | Advantage | Risk / required control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed project fee | Stable scope and launch | Buyer knows budget; rewards efficiency | Tight change control, assumptions and acceptance criteria |
| Paid direction sprint | Unclear concept, assets or technology | Reduces unpaid speculation and bad fixed quotes | Must produce a useful decision even if production stops |
| Edition fee | Annual event, exhibition, seasonal launch | Creates repeatability without a generic retainer | Define what changes and what reuses |
| Burst retainer | PR/launch window with variable assets | Reserves capacity through campaign volatility | Hours/days, response time and out-of-scope work must be capped |
| Reserved-capacity retainer | Agency, label, hospitality group | Smoother revenue and repeat collaboration | No “unlimited design”; specify slots, expiry and priority |
| Build-only fixed fee | Approved external design | Clear role and partner fit | Responsive/accessibility gaps in supplied design need a correction mechanism |
| Specialist pass-through | 3D, film, sound, illustration, translation | Right expert for each world | Markup/management fee and usage rights must be transparent |
| Care plan | Stable live site | Security, monitoring and small updates | Separate defects from new creative work |

### Quote construction

**Planning formula:**

> Reserved senior capacity + production complexity + coordination/risk + specialist/asset cost + contingency + launch support.

Do not quote from page count alone. A one-page fragrance experience can be more demanding than a ten-page editorial site.

Quote variables:

- number and maturity of concepts;
- amount and readiness of content;
- original asset production;
- interaction/rendering complexity;
- integration and data risk;
- languages and markets;
- accessibility and compliance review;
- stakeholder count;
- deadline rigidity;
- expected traffic and launch support;
- archive/maintenance requirements;
- case-study and credit rights.

### Suggested payment and change discipline

**Planning hypothesis:**

- 40–50% non-refundable booking payment;
- 25–30% at approved direction/master frame;
- balance before production deployment or source transfer;
- third-party costs prepaid;
- work pauses on overdue invoices or missing approvals;
- late client inputs move the launch unless a written recovery plan is purchased;
- cancellation fee reflects completed work and reserved capacity;
- one consolidated feedback set per round;
- change requests receive a schedule/fee impact before work begins.

The final terms require Indonesian legal/accounting review.

## Duration model

| Workstream | Lean condition | Typical planning allowance | Expansion trigger |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Fit/scoping | Clear brief and assets | 2–3 days elapsed | Multiple stakeholders or unknown platform |
| Direction sprint | One decision maker | 1–2 weeks | Research, naming, prototype or new asset method |
| Content/asset production | Existing campaign assets | 1–3 weeks parallel | Original shoot, illustration, 3D, sound, rights or translation |
| Experience design | Approved world and content map | 2–4 weeks | Multiple routes, data states or complex mobile reinterpretation |
| Development | Stable design/assets | 2–6 weeks | WebGL, CMS, personalization, third-party API or heavy media |
| QA/launch | Conventional integration | 1 week | Localization, high traffic, institutional review, event-day support |

**Inference:** “Delivered in ten days” cases exist, but speed is usually enabled by narrow scope, ready assets, decisive approvals, reuse, or high parallel capacity. It should not become a default promise for authored work.

## Path from invitations to commercial microsites

This path is a portfolio and relationship ladder, not an automatic market progression.

### Step 1 — Prove the full craft on one occasion

Create one authored invitation world with:

- genuine concept;
- strong assets;
- mobile-first guest journey;
- RSVP integration;
- print/share extension;
- accessible and static alternatives;
- credits and process.

**Evidence gained:** House Adel can turn story into a complete, shipped, time-bound experience.

### Step 2 — Win a planner or venue channel

Package the process, not the aesthetic:

- planner intake;
- asset/content checklist;
- approval calendar;
- integration choices;
- clear customization boundary;
- reliable launch and guest support handoff.

**Evidence gained:** repeatable delivery without repeating the look.

### Step 3 — Expand from invitation to event identity

Add announcement, itinerary, venue screens, signage rules, social motion, and archive for a public or sponsored event.

**Evidence gained:** one concept can govern multiple touchpoints and changing content.

### Step 4 — Enter hospitality through the shared ecosystem

Use venue, planner, photographer, and event-producer relationships to approach a restaurant, villa, hotel, or cultural opening. Sell launch story plus booking/menu/location integration.

**Evidence gained:** House Adel can create desire for a commercial place and connect it to an operational action.

### Step 5 — Partner into a branded launch

Work under a brand, PR, or design agency that already owns client strategy, assets, media and approvals. Take a bounded digital concept/build role.

**Evidence gained:** commercial stakeholder management, analytics, brand governance, traffic and campaign deadlines.

### Step 6 — Sell direct compact launches selectively

Only after case studies prove outcomes, roles, partner credits, fallbacks, and launch reliability should House Adel pursue direct product/release campaign ownership.

**Evidence gained:** a credible studio proposition beyond invitations.

### What must remain constant through the ladder

- Senior art direction;
- asset-first process;
- one governing idea;
- semantic access to essential actions;
- honest capability/credits;
- production and rights records;
- mobile and reduced-motion reinterpretation;
- launch discipline.

The visual language should not remain constant. Reusing one “House Adel look” across a wedding, exhibition, artist release, and fragrance launch would contradict the studio premise.

## Validation plan before publishing offers or prices

Run 8–12 structured conversations:

- 2–3 wedding/event planners or luxury venues;
- 1–2 artist managers/labels;
- 1–2 curators/galleries/cultural producers;
- 1–2 hospitality founders or brand designers;
- 2–3 PR, brand, event, or advertising agencies.

Ask for evidence, not compliments:

1. What did the last comparable launch/event buy?
2. Who owned the budget and selected suppliers?
3. Which deliverables were indispensable?
4. What failed or created coordination cost?
5. What was built in-house versus externally?
6. What did the website have to integrate?
7. When was the supplier appointed relative to launch?
8. Which fee structure was easiest to approve?
9. What proof would make a new Indonesian studio credible?
10. Would they buy direct, refer, or white-label—and why?

Then test 3–5 real proposals with scope and fee corridors. Record loss reasons. Do not lower price automatically when the actual issue is trust, proof, timing, or buyer fit.

## Decision gates for the creative director

The following remain unapproved:

- which service model is the lead offer;
- whether invitations appear as a primary category, a sub-offer, or only a case-study type;
- which quote-test corridor is viable;
- direct-client versus partner revenue balance;
- use of “atelier,” “studio,” or “creative development” language;
- whether the multiverse portfolio concept clarifies or distracts from the chosen market;
- which specimen project should be produced first.
