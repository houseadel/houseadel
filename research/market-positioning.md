# House Adel market-positioning research

**Status:** Phase 1 evidence and strategic options; no positioning selected  
**Research date:** 2026-07-30  
**Source access date:** All web sources were accessed 2026-07-30 unless a different date is stated.  
**Naming:** This document uses **House Adel**, never “House of Adel.”

## How to read this document

- **Evidence** means a claim is supported by the primary or authoritative source linked beside it.
- **Inference** means a conclusion drawn from several observations but not stated by a source.
- **Planning hypothesis** means an assumption House Adel can test in sales conversations. It is not a market fact or an approved offer.
- **Direction decision** means a choice reserved for the creative director.

Public price evidence is strong for software, templates, and Indonesian invitation vendors, but sparse for bespoke creative studios. Studio Freight, Locomotive, OFF+BRAND, Stink Studios, and UNIT9 publish services and case studies but not rate cards. One useful small-studio exception is Rhumb Studio, which publishes fixed-fee starting points of about €2,000 for a landing-page sprint and €12,000 for full strategy/design/build work. [Rhumb Studio](https://www.rhumb.co/) This report therefore documents pricing *structures* confidently, uses public figures only where they exist, and labels all proposed House Adel figures as hypotheses.

## Executive findings

1. **The functional website is already a commodity.** A couple can get a wedding website, RSVP, hosting, guest management, and templates for free through Joy; Minted supplies a free site and charges only $15 for a custom URL; Linkfire sells music-release landing pages and analytics from $21 per month on annual billing; Squarespace and Wix bundle restaurant pages, menus, reservations, and commerce into site builders. Sources: [Joy pricing](https://withjoy.com/pricing/), [Minted wedding websites](https://www.minted.com/lp/wedding-websites), [Linkfire pricing](https://www.linkfire.com/pricing), [Squarespace restaurant websites](https://www.squarespace.com/tour/restaurant-websites), [Wix Restaurants](https://www.wix.com/restaurant/website).
2. **“More customization” is not enough to escape the commodity tier.** Design-forward wedding platforms already sell coordinated templates, custom domains, multi-language support, advanced RSVP, privacy, and stationery: Bliss & Bone starts at $15/month, while Riley & Grey is $39/month or $299/year. Sources: [Bliss & Bone features and pricing](https://blissandbone.com/wedding-website/features-and-pricing), [Riley & Grey pricing](https://www.rileygrey.com/pricing).
3. **The premium layer is authorship across a moment, not a decorated page.** Strong event, culture, and launch cases join a governing idea to motion, content, physical or campaign touchpoints, and operational goals. Studio Dumbar’s DEMO identity extended from a generative variable type system into the website, trailers, and 80 station screens; Linked by Air built a curatorial interaction model that could support Cooper Hewitt’s future exhibitions; Stink Studios delivered a Chanel experience across mobile, tablet, 24 languages, display advertising, and a China-specific activation. Sources: [DEMO 2019](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo), [Cooper Hewitt digital exhibitions](https://www.linkedbyair.net/work/132-cooper-hewitt-smithsonian-design-museum), [Chanel Chance](https://www.stinkstudios.com/work/chance).
4. **Digital invitations are a credible entry wedge but a weak final category.** They fit House Adel’s current strengths and create a practical portfolio of mobile storytelling, typography, imagery, motion, RSVP, and event logistics. Yet local vendors sell feature-rich products from Rp79,000–Rp499,000, and even custom-design upgrades are visible as line items. Competing on “a beautiful digital invitation” alone would force comparison with a large, cheap supply. Sources: [Wedera pricing](https://www.wedera.id/), [Wedly pricing](https://wedly.id/), [Connectied proposal](https://connectied.id/proposal.pdf).
5. **“Focused digital experiences” is a useful internal scope but an underspecified market promise.** A buyer still needs to know *for whom* and *for what moment*. “Launches, releases, exhibitions, and occasions” makes the focus concrete without committing House Adel to one vertical. **Inference:** that phrase should be tested as an explanatory line, not treated as a self-sufficient category.
6. **The realistic international route is partnership-led.** House Adel is more likely to begin with affluent private clients, wedding and event planners, boutique hospitality founders, cultural producers, independent artist teams, brand designers, PR firms, and agencies needing a creative-development partner than with Chanel, Netflix, Spotify, or a national museum directly. Bali already has an organized professional wedding ecosystem spanning planners, luxury venues, photographers, caterers, and decorators, and Indonesia’s official tourism site specifically advises foreign couples to hire local planners. Sources: [Bali Wedding Association member directory](https://baliweddingassociation.com/member-directory/), [Indonesia Travel destination-wedding guidance](https://www.indonesia.travel/in/en/travel-ideas/adventure/your-dream-wedding-in-bali-top-venues-unique-features-and-tips-for-foreigners).
7. **House Adel should not rebuild specialist infrastructure.** RSVP databases, ticketing, reservations, music pre-saves, streaming links, art inventory, and ecommerce are mature products. The studio can create the authored layer and integrate those systems. Sources: [Paperless Post professional features](https://www.paperlesspost.com/pricing), [Spotify Countdown Pages](https://artists.spotify.com/countdown-pages), [Artlogic](https://artlogic.net/), [Tock](https://www.squarespace.com/tock).

## The actual market: seven buying situations

| Buying situation | What the buyer is trying to achieve | Commodity baseline | Premium reason to commission | Realistic initial buyer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wedding or private occasion | Inform guests, collect responses, set the emotional tone | Template site, RSVP, map, registry, gallery, WhatsApp link | One authored world tied to the couple, venue, dress, photography, and event identity | Destination-wedding couple through a planner; private host; luxury venue |
| Public event or festival | Sell tickets, publish program and logistics, make the event recognizable | Event page, schedule, registration, email | A repeatable identity system across web, social, motion, stage, signage, and future editions | Independent festival, conference, creative community, event producer |
| Artist or release | Concentrate attention, convert anticipation into streams, tickets, merch, or owned fan data | Smart link, pre-save, countdown, DSP page | A release world, participatory mechanic, editorial narrative, or press-worthy digital artifact | Independent label, management team, established regional artist, culture brand |
| Exhibition or cultural program | Extend curatorial thinking online, improve access, document work | CMS, artwork database, viewing room, standard exhibition pages | A digital exhibition with its own spatial or editorial logic and a durable archive | Gallery, independent curator, foundation, festival, small museum program |
| Restaurant or hospitality opening | Create desire and enable discovery, menu viewing, booking, directions | Restaurant template, reservation integration, menu, SEO | Translate place, food, materials, service ritual, and launch story into one experience | New chef-led restaurant, boutique hotel/villa, hospitality group, destination venue |
| Luxury, beauty, or fragrance launch | Make an intangible product world felt and move audiences toward retail | Campaign landing page, film embed, product detail, retailer links | Sensory storytelling, high-end assets, interaction, localization, and campaign extensions | Independent fragrance/beauty house; regional distributor; PR or brand agency |
| Compact brand campaign | Give one proposition, launch, anniversary, or cultural moment its own container | Landing-page builder and ad destination | A concentrated interactive idea with its own assets, pacing, participation, and measurement | Funded startup, brand team, agency, production partner, entertainment property |

## 1. Premium digital invitations and wedding microsites

### Evidence: the baseline is broad and inexpensive

- Joy offers a website, hosting, unlimited guests, online RSVP, planning dashboard, registry, app, and hundreds of digital invites for $0; it monetizes registry transactions, optional domains, messaging, and premium stationery. [Joy pricing](https://withjoy.com/pricing/)
- Minted offers free wedding sites with guest lists, RSVP, privacy, registry links, matching stationery, and a $15 custom URL upgrade. [Minted wedding websites](https://www.minted.com/lp/wedding-websites)
- Bliss & Bone’s design-forward templates start at $15/month for Standard and $21/month for Premium and include responsive behavior, RSVP, custom-domain connection, private events, multi-language support, and coordinated stationery. [Bliss & Bone features and pricing](https://blissandbone.com/wedding-website/features-and-pricing)
- Riley & Grey charges $39/month or $299/year for publication, a custom domain, updates, digital invitations, registry, and support. It explicitly competes on design, service, privacy, and an ad-free experience rather than missing functionality. [Riley & Grey pricing](https://www.rileygrey.com/pricing)
- Indonesia has intense low-price supply. Wedera lists Rp79,000 and Rp129,000 packages with RSVP, maps, story, countdown, music, gallery, and unlimited sharing; Wedly lists Rp349,000 and Rp499,000 packages with dashboard, unlimited guests, custom colors/fonts, media, gifts, and one-year availability. [Wedera](https://www.wedera.id/), [Wedly](https://wedly.id/)
- Connectied’s published proposal makes the customization ladder unusually visible: custom template +Rp850,000; custom design +Rp2,100,000; custom design with an illustrator +Rp5,250,000; design exclusivity +Rp2,100,000; and custom domain +Rp400,000/year. [Connectied proposal](https://connectied.id/proposal.pdf)

### What is commoditized

Guest-name personalization, WhatsApp sharing, a countdown, music, a photo gallery, maps, streaming embeds, RSVP, guest wishes, digital gifts, a subdomain, basic animation, and theme color/font changes are feature-table items. They are not credible differentiators.

### What remains premium

- A concept derived from the people and occasion rather than from a theme library.
- An asset world: commissioned or directed photography, illustration, typography, film, sound, and culturally specific material with provenance.
- A coherent suite spanning save-the-date, invitation, website, itinerary, venue screens, printed matter, social crops, and post-event archive.
- Guest segmentation, bilingual or multilingual content, private-event logic, accessibility, and calm support for an international guest list.
- A planner-facing production process that reduces coordination rather than making the couple manage a web project.

**Inference:** “premium” must be proven by visible art-direction decisions and service, not adjectives, gold color, music autoplay, or more animation.

### Market judgment

**Digital invitations are not too narrow as a launch wedge.** They are too narrow as the studio’s permanent market definition. The buyer is usually one-off, the comparison set is price-compressed, and the work can be mistaken for template customization. The stronger wedge is an **occasion identity and guest experience**, of which the site is one important surface.

### Realistic entry

The Bali Wedding Association directory shows a dense partnership network of professional planners, resort venues, photographers, decorators, and caterers; it is a more plausible route to affluent and international couples than direct performance advertising against Rp99,000 products. [Bali Wedding Association directory](https://baliweddingassociation.com/member-directory/)

**Planning hypothesis:** begin with one or two deliberately selected planner/venue relationships, a private white-label option, and one exceptional self-initiated invitation specimen. Do not build a template catalogue.

## 2. Event identity and event websites

### Evidence: buyers need a system, not only a site

Studio Dumbar’s DEMO case connects strategy, motion identity, a self-developed variable-type script, an interactive website, trailers, and the live event across 80 screens. [Studio Dumbar, DEMO 2019](https://studiodumbar.com/work/demo) Porto Rocha’s Netflix Is a Joke identity was designed to maintain continuity from promotion through an 11-day festival with 280 events and a very broad performer lineup. [PORTO ROCHA, Netflix Is a Joke](https://portorocha.com/netflixisajoke/)

Paperless Post demonstrates the operational commodity layer: event pages, custom URL, password protection, guest tags, surveys, check-in, analytics, messaging, and professional annual plans starting at $250/year for 250 annual guests. [Paperless Post pricing](https://www.paperlesspost.com/pricing)

### What clients buy

- Event premise and identity.
- Flexible visual and motion system.
- Announcement, ticket-release, speaker/artist reveal, countdown, and live-update states.
- Program, speakers, venues, maps, accessibility, travel, partners, and ticket or registration integration.
- Social launch kit, email graphics, stage/screen assets, signage rules, and post-event archive.
- For recurring events, a governed edition system that changes each year without losing recognition.

### Premium value

The website is the operational hub, but the premium commission is the authored system that survives across campaign cadence and physical space. A one-off spectacle that cannot accommodate schedule changes, sponsor marks, a sold-out state, or the next edition has weak commercial value.

### Repeat potential

High for annual festivals, conferences, awards, and recurring cultural programs if House Adel supplies the edition system, launch assets, web updates, and post-event archive. The event identity can lead to a seasonal or annual retainer.

## 3. Artist-release and music microsites

### Evidence: release utility is already productized

- Spotify Countdown Pages combine pre-save, tracklist, video Clips, merch, notification, discovery placement, and performance statistics inside the listening platform. Spotify reports that more than 60% of listeners who pre-save an album stream it in week one. [Spotify for Artists, Countdown Pages](https://artists.spotify.com/countdown-pages)
- Linkfire sells unlimited release, pre-release, bio, ticket, content, and reward links with branding, routing, pixels, email collection, and analytics from $21/month on annual billing; its Teams plan is $45/month annually. [Linkfire pricing](https://www.linkfire.com/pricing)
- Music Glue combines branded artist stores, music, merchandise, tickets, fan data, pre-order campaigns, and ticket presales for more than 30,000 artists. [Music Glue](https://www.musicglue.com/)

### Consequence for a bespoke studio

House Adel should not sell “a pre-save page.” It should use Linkfire, Spotify, Music Glue, ticketing, and ecommerce as destinations or embeds while selling the part they do not provide: a distinct release world, narrative, participatory mechanic, collectible digital artifact, or editorial layer.

UNIT9’s Coke Studio project shows the future-scale ceiling: a roamable 3D record store containing artist-specific games around an international music campaign. [UNIT9, Coke Studio Virtual Record Store](https://www.unit9.com/project/coke-studio-real-magic-virtual-record-store) It is evidence that brands and labels buy interactive music worlds, but it is a **future-scale production reference**, not a realistic initial scope for one generalist.

### Realistic first clients

- An established Indonesian or Southeast Asian artist with management, a label, a tour, and a visual identity already in production.
- An independent label releasing several records per year.
- A fashion, beverage, or culture brand collaborating with an artist.
- A creative director or label designer who needs implementation and interaction.

**Inference:** emerging artists without management or a campaign budget are more likely to use Linkfire and social media. They may be creatively aligned but are poor core clients unless a label, sponsor, grant, or in-kind portfolio decision funds the work.

### Repeat potential

Medium to high when the relationship is with management or a label: singles, albums, tours, merch drops, archives, and anniversary editions recur. Low when contracted by an unfunded artist for one release.

## 4. Exhibition and cultural websites

### Evidence: operational platforms are mature

Artlogic combines artwork inventory, CRM, websites, marketing, payments, and sales for more than 6,000 galleries, artists, and collectors; it offers both synchronized sites and a custom design studio. [Artlogic platform](https://artlogic.net/) Its viewing rooms can exchange time-sensitive exhibition access for lead data. [Artlogic Viewing Rooms](https://support.artlogic.net/hc/en-gb/articles/360010025920-Introduction-to-Viewing-Rooms)

### Evidence: curatorial interaction can justify bespoke work

Linked by Air did not simply reskin a collection grid for Cooper Hewitt. It built a WordPress exhibition platform whose two-door navigation, randomized paths, journey map, search, and reusable structure reflect both physical exploration and future exhibition needs. [Linked by Air, Cooper Hewitt](https://www.linkedbyair.net/work/132-cooper-hewitt-smithsonian-design-museum)

### What clients buy

- Digital translation of a curatorial thesis.
- Exhibition introduction, works, artists, essays, chronology, media, credits, access information, and archive.
- An interaction or editorial structure appropriate to the material.
- CMS or repeatable platform when more than one exhibition will use it.
- Rights-aware media handling, captions, transcripts, alt text, and durable URLs.
- On-site QR, kiosk, projection, or screen extensions where relevant.

### Premium and risk

Premium value lies in interpretation, access, and durable documentation. Cultural institutions often need procurement, rights clearance, scholarly content, translations, accessibility review, and stakeholder approval. **Inference:** a “small exhibition site” can carry more governance and content risk than a visually larger commercial launch.

### Realistic entry

Independent curators, galleries, foundations, university programs, design festivals, artist estates, and small institutions are more realistic than national museums. Partnering with a curator, exhibition designer, or cultural brand studio reduces the risk that House Adel is asked to invent curatorial content.

## 5. Restaurant and hospitality openings

### Evidence: operational needs are standardized

Squarespace and Wix provide restaurant templates, menu structures, reservations, ordering, marketing, payments, and responsive behavior. [Squarespace restaurant websites](https://www.squarespace.com/tour/restaurant-websites), [Wix Restaurants](https://www.wix.com/restaurant/website) BentoBox positions a higher-service version of the same layer: consultation, hospitality-specific templates, customization, onboarding, accessibility/SEO practices, and ongoing maintenance. [BentoBox restaurant website design](https://www.getbento.com/features/restaurant-website-design/) Tock covers reservations, event tickets, table management, waitlists, guest CRM, payments, and integrations. [Tock](https://www.squarespace.com/tock)

### What bespoke work must add

- A premise rooted in the place, chef, ingredients, service ritual, architecture, landscape, or host.
- Launch art direction and an asset plan before interface styling.
- Photography/film direction, menu and story treatment, launch countdown, press material, and social crops.
- Frictionless access to hours, location, menu, reservation, accessibility, contact, and language.
- Integration with Tock, OpenTable, WhatsApp, a local booking provider, or the venue’s existing stack.

Locomotive’s work list includes The Drake Hotel as a digital experience and content engagement, alongside strategy-to-deployment capability. It illustrates the production tier above a restaurant template, but the agency’s team and breadth make it a **future-scale reference**. [Locomotive work](https://locomotive.ca/en/work), [Locomotive agency and capabilities](https://locomotive.ca/en/agency)

### Realistic first clients and repeat

Chef-led restaurants before opening, boutique hotels and villas, destination wedding venues, hospitality groups launching a new property, and brand designers already creating the physical identity. Repeat work can include seasonal editions, new menus, events, new properties, and campaign assets. A single independent restaurant with no photography or launch budget is a weak fit.

## 6. Fragrance, beauty, and luxury launches

### Evidence: premium work is campaign infrastructure

Stink Studios’ Chanel Chance case included strategy, creative concept, interactive film, mobile/tablet support, 24-language localization, display advertising, a YouTube masthead, and a China-specific experience. [Stink Studios, Chance](https://www.stinkstudios.com/work/chance) A Trussardi Primo case combines an interactive 360° Lanzarote site, campaign visuals, product notes, contest/data collection, social promotion, and a branded Fortnite experience. [Xister Reply, Trussardi Primo](https://www.xister.com/works/trussardi-parfums-primo-digital-launch/)

These are **future-scale references**. They demonstrate the buying unit—concept plus assets, technology, localization, media, retail path, analytics, and launch coordination—not a reasonable first-project scope for House Adel.

### Where House Adel can enter

- An independent fragrance, beauty, jewelry, fashion, or object brand with one hero product and strong founders.
- A regional distributor that needs a localized launch layer.
- A PR, brand, or campaign agency that holds strategy/media and needs a digital craft partner.
- A photographer/director who has the film and stills but needs an interactive container.

### Premium value and asset burden

Fragrance has no screen-native sensory output. The value comes from metaphor, image, motion, sound, material behavior, and pacing. This makes it especially aligned with House Adel’s interests and especially dependent on excellent assets. AI-assisted assets may support exploration and selected production, but usage rights, product accuracy, human approval, and visible craft are prerequisites.

## 7. Compact campaign experiences

RIOT defines microsites as focused digital experiences for one campaign, launch, or moment—smaller than a full site but more concentrated in tone, narrative, and purpose. [RIOT microsite service](https://riot.nyc/services/digital/microsites/) This is close to the structural market House Adel is considering.

### Typical deliverables

- Brief clarification and one governing idea.
- Art direction and master frame.
- One responsive destination with a small number of purposeful routes or chapters.
- Motion/interaction, product or story content, and a clear conversion.
- Integration with CRM, ecommerce, booking, streaming, ticketing, or a main site.
- Analytics plan, campaign tags, consent behavior, share metadata, SEO basics.
- Launch QA, reduced-motion and static fallbacks, handover, and a defined sunset/archive plan.
- Optional campaign extensions: teaser, social crops, motion loops, OOH/screen adaptations, email headers, press kit.

### What buyers pay for

Speed to a fixed launch date, a distinctive concept, senior attention, asset orchestration, reliable implementation, and low coordination cost. “WebGL” or “award-winning” is not itself a business outcome.

### Repeat potential

High with brand, PR, entertainment, and design-agency relationships because launches recur. The challenge is schedule volatility and dependence on assets and approvals from other teams.

## Commoditized value versus defensible value

| Layer | Commodity or rapidly commoditizing | Defensible premium value |
|---|---|---|
| Information | Date, time, venue, menu, track links, product copy | Editorial hierarchy and a narrative specific to the moment |
| Utility | RSVP, registration, countdown, gallery, map, booking, pre-save | Integrating utilities without breaking the authored experience |
| Visuals | Template palette, stock image, generic animation | Directed asset world with provenance and a recognizable premise |
| Interaction | Smooth scroll, hover distortion, particles, custom cursor | Interaction that explains, reveals, or lets the audience participate in the idea |
| Service | Form intake and quick theme setup | Senior creative direction, curation, production stewardship, calm launch support |
| System | One disposable page | Reusable edition/release system where recurrence matters |
| Risk | Basic hosting and device responsiveness | Rights, localization, accessibility, fallbacks, QA, analytics, and launch reliability |
| Outcome | “Has a website” | Builds anticipation, recognition, understanding, attendance, conversion, or cultural memory |

## Who can afford bespoke work

This report does not infer wealth from aesthetic taste. A plausible buyer has both a meaningful moment and an organizational reason to invest:

- A destination-wedding couple already coordinating multiple specialist vendors through a planner.
- A venue or planner that can amortize a digital system across premium packages.
- An event with sponsors, ticket revenue, an annual cycle, or a communications budget.
- An artist with management, label, sponsor, tour, merch, or grant support.
- A gallery/foundation with an exhibition communications budget and cleared content.
- A funded hospitality opening where identity, interiors, photography, PR, and reservations are already being commissioned.
- A product brand with retail distribution and a launch campaign.
- An agency with a client-approved production budget.

**Inference:** “People who appreciate design” is not a target segment. Evidence of a budgeted launch, professional team, deadline, and distribution plan is a better qualification signal.

## Pricing evidence and likely structures

### What is directly observable

| Market layer | Public evidence | What it indicates |
|---|---|---|
| Free wedding utility | Joy $0; Minted $0 plus $15 custom URL | RSVP, hosting, templates and guest information have almost no standalone price power |
| Design-forward wedding platform | Bliss & Bone $15–$21/month; Riley & Grey $39/month or $299/year | Curation, privacy, coordinated stationery and support create a modest paid tier, still based on reused systems |
| Indonesian invitation service | Wedera Rp79k–Rp129k; Wedly Rp349k–Rp499k | Feature-rich local supply is highly price-compressed |
| Indonesian custom upgrade | Connectied custom design +Rp2.1m; custom illustration +Rp5.25m | Original visual production can command a visible premium, but remains an add-on within an operational invitation offer |
| Music campaign utility | Linkfire Pro $21/month annually; Teams $45/month annually | Routing, pre-save, analytics and fan-capture utility are subscription software |
| Professional event utility | Paperless Post Pro starts at $250/year for 250 annual guests | Repeat event operations support annual per-guest pricing |
| Small creative web studio | Rhumb landing-page sprint near €2k; full strategy/design/build starts near €12k, fixed fee | A realistic independent peer prices the authored service orders of magnitude above templates and sells a gated end-to-end process |
| Integrated or global creative studio | Official sites reviewed use inquiry/contact rather than public rate cards | Bespoke campaign pricing is negotiated against scope, team, assets, markets and risk; no defensible median follows from the public evidence |

Sources: [Joy](https://withjoy.com/pricing/), [Minted](https://www.minted.com/lp/wedding-websites), [Bliss & Bone](https://blissandbone.com/wedding-website/features-and-pricing), [Riley & Grey](https://www.rileygrey.com/pricing), [Wedera](https://www.wedera.id/), [Wedly](https://wedly.id/), [Connectied](https://connectied.id/proposal.pdf), [Linkfire](https://www.linkfire.com/pricing), [Paperless Post](https://www.paperlesspost.com/pricing), [Rhumb](https://www.rhumb.co/).

### Likely bespoke structures

- **Fixed project fee** after scope and assets are defined: most appropriate for one bounded occasion, edition or launch.
- **Paid direction/feasibility sprint:** appropriate when the concept, content, assets or technology are not ready for a reliable fixed quote.
- **Edition fee:** appropriate for annual events, gallery seasons, repeat releases and hospitality chapters that reuse an operating system but need new art direction.
- **Burst retainer:** appropriate around a launch window with variable campaign assets and updates.
- **Reserved-capacity retainer:** appropriate for agencies, labels, planners or hospitality groups with recurring work; it requires a hard capacity ceiling.
- **Specialist and asset pass-through:** 3D, illustration, film, sound, translation, licensing, messaging and print should be explicit rather than hidden inside “website design.”
- **Care/archive fee:** appropriate only where the experience remains live, needs updates, or must be preserved.

**Inference:** a low public “starting at” price would likely attract template comparisons before House Adel has enough cases to explain the difference. Proposal-based quote tests are safer during Phase 1.

## An Indonesian independent studio working internationally

### Advantages

- **Regional access:** Bali’s professional wedding, venue, hospitality, and production ecosystem gives House Adel a local route to internationally oriented clients. [Bali Wedding Association directory](https://baliweddingassociation.com/member-directory/)
- **Direct founder involvement:** the creative director remains close to the concept and work rather than passing it through account layers.
- **A partner-shaped team:** specialists can be assembled only when a world needs 3D, film, sound, illustration, copy, or advanced engineering.
- **Time-zone position:** Jakarta overlaps with Southeast Asia and part of Australia, while async review can serve Europe and North America.
- **Cultural range:** Indonesian and Southeast Asian references can be a source of specific knowledge when used accurately, not an “exotic” style device.

### Weaknesses

- No current evidence yet of repeated international delivery, outcomes, or complex launches.
- One person cannot credibly promise senior art direction, custom 3D, film, sound, advanced WebGL, production management, and engineering at once.
- International buyers may worry about contracting, payment, support windows, language, and recourse.
- AI-assisted production can trigger quality, rights, and brand-safety concerns without a rigorous provenance system.
- A visually experimental portfolio can imply high risk if it omits process, accessibility, mobile behavior, and delivery detail.

### Operating model required

- English-first proposal, statement of work, schedule, named approver, revision limits, acceptance criteria, cancellation terms, IP/license terms, third-party costs, and post-launch responsibility.
- Fixed milestones and deposits; no large speculative build before concept approval.
- Async review packets with a scheduled overlap window rather than pretending to provide every-time-zone availability.
- Currency and payment method confirmed before signing. Wise’s Indonesia availability page currently shows outbound international transfer availability and its Indonesia business-verification page says registration is currently limited to freelancers; therefore receipt features must be verified for the actual legal entity rather than promised from a generic marketing page. [Wise availability in Indonesia](https://wise.com/id/availability/), [Wise Indonesia business verification](https://wise.com/id/help/articles/3BQmjvNODjPGKkmIUx5dsR/bagaimana-cara-memverifikasi-bisnis-saya-di-indonesia)
- Indonesian accountant and counsel review for tax, invoicing, entity, foreign-currency, privacy, and contract obligations. This report is not legal or tax advice.
- Named specialist partners and transparent credits. Do not present partner capabilities as permanent in-house capacity.

## Advantages, weaknesses, and market risks

### Advantages inherent in the proposed scope

- Compact experiences can concentrate craft without requiring large application backends.
- Invitations, launches, releases, and exhibitions all reward typography, photography direction, composition, pacing, sound, and story.
- The same technical primitives—content schema, asset manifests, route transitions, integrations, fallbacks, analytics, and launch QA—can be reused without making projects look alike.
- A small senior-led studio can be attractive when clients value direct collaboration and the project is bounded.

### Structural weaknesses

- Each project can look unique enough that estimating and reuse become difficult.
- Asset quality often matters more than code; a client without assets can expand scope dramatically.
- One-off launches create feast-or-famine scheduling.
- Awards-oriented references can distort client expectations toward spectacle and away from useful content.
- Bespoke work competes with almost-free tools, so qualification and explanation must be excellent.

### Market risks and mitigations

| Risk | Consequence | Mitigation to test |
|---|---|---|
| Invitation price anchoring | A bespoke quote is compared with Rp99,000 templates | Sell through planners; name the offer as occasion identity/experience; show suite-wide deliverables |
| Category vagueness | “Focused digital experiences” sounds like any web studio | Always name the moments and show 3–4 concrete flagship deliverables |
| Capability overreach | Advanced assets or WebGL exceed one-person capacity | Scope asset-first; use paid specialists; offer a cinematic 2.5D alternative |
| Launch-date compression | Quality/accessibility suffers | Paid rush tier, asset freeze, approver map, kill criteria, static fallback |
| Weak repeat revenue | Constant new-business burden | Planner/agency partnerships, annual editions, label rosters, launch retainers, care plans |
| Client-supplied asset failure | The interface cannot rescue weak imagery | Paid art-direction/asset phase; approval gate before build |
| AI sameness or rights uncertainty | Reputational and legal risk | Provenance records, licensed inputs, human approval, no unverifiable media |
| International trust gap | Lost deals or unfavorable terms | Clear contracts, references, staged payments, documented QA, credits, English case studies |
| White-label invisibility | Revenue grows but public portfolio does not | Negotiate delayed case-study or anonymized process rights; balance direct and partner work |
| Luxury imitation | Work looks expensive but not specific | Derive the world from product/place/story; reject generic black/gold, bloom, particles, and perfume-cloud clichés |

## Research conclusion, not a final positioning

The evidence supports a **market shape**, not one permanent label:

- Keep invitations as a credible proving ground and referral wedge.
- Move the object of sale upward from “website” to an authored moment and its digital system.
- Integrate mature operational platforms instead of recreating them.
- Use partnerships to reach budgeted clients and fill specialist gaps.
- Qualify projects by moment, assets, budget, team, deadline, and distribution—not by industry prestige.
- Make the initial category concrete enough to buy and broad enough to progress from occasions into releases, exhibitions, hospitality, and compact launches.

The creative director still needs to choose which customer and moment House Adel wants to be known for first.
