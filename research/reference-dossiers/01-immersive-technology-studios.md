# Immersive and creative-technology studios

Status: comparative evidence for Phase 1; these are mostly future-scale references, not direct operating-model peers.  
Access date: 2026-07-30.  
Evidence note: implementation is named only where an official source documents it. `Inference` marks interpretation.

## Active Theory

**Classification:** future-scale creative-technology reference; narrow technical reference.

**Business layer.** Active Theory describes itself as a “Creative Technology & Experience Studio” founded in 2012. Its company-maintained [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/active-theory) lists an in-house 11–50-person range and capabilities across creative direction, technical direction, frontend/backend work, campaigns, and installations. Because LinkedIn is company-maintained rather than an independent audit, the range should be treated as directional. The current [official portfolio](https://activetheory.net/) is JavaScript-dependent and provides little indexable service detail without running the experience.

**Governing concept and durable value.** The studio’s durable idea is not a single visual metaphor; it is technology as a medium for shared experience. The useful evidence appears in its studio-authored project and technology writing. Its [technology history](https://medium.com/active-theory/the-story-of-technology-built-at-active-theory-5d17ae0e3fb4) explains how repeated project demands led to Hydra, Hydra GUI, and the Aura native wrapper. The result after the first 30 seconds is a production thesis: build infrastructure when recurring creative constraints justify it.

**Navigation, motion, rendering, and assets.** The public portfolio currently asks the visitor to run JavaScript and foregrounds an experiential work layer. Exact current portfolio navigation, renderer, CMS, and mobile policy are not documented in the sources used here. Historical studio-authored evidence is specific:

- [Paper Planes](https://medium.com/active-theory/paper-planes-6b0008c56c17) documents a JavaScript/WebGL codebase shared across a physical installation and mobile participation, Web Workers for flocking, mobile reductions in thread and plane counts, and WebGL-rendered text.
- [Mira](https://medium.com/active-theory/mira-exploring-the-potential-of-the-future-web-e1f7f326d58e) describes Hydra routing and WebGL, with effects reduced across device tiers.
- [Neon](https://medium.com/active-theory/neon-a-webgl-installation-fdf540c42152) documents a WebGL installation.
- [How I Fight](https://medium.com/active-theory/how-i-fight-stand-up-to-cancer-cf0ab227b44e) discusses an optimized shader-based mask.

These are project-specific or historical claims. They do not prove that Hydra renders the current portfolio.

**Project presentation and production.** The authored articles are stronger evidence than the portfolio entrance because they describe the brief, technical problem, tradeoffs, device behavior, and system. That translation from spectacle into production judgment is reusable. `Inference:` the asset and specialist burden is high, based on the company’s stated cross-disciplinary capabilities and installation work rather than an undocumented guess about a particular page.

**Mobile and accessibility.** Paper Planes and Mira document device-tier reductions, which is performance evidence rather than an accessibility audit. Current keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, no-WebGL, and fallback behavior remain unverified.

**House Adel assessment.** Reuse the principle that internal technology should emerge from repeated needs. Do not use a custom engine, “microverse,” or persistent canvas as a status signal. Active Theory is most relevant as evidence that ambitious creative technology requires a pipeline and an operating history, not merely an art direction.

## Resn

**Classification:** future-scale expressive studio reference.

**Business layer.** Resn’s current [official home](https://resn.co.nz/) and [work page](https://resn.co.nz/work) foreground discovery, a showreel, audio, and a project view. The company-maintained [Behance profile](https://www.behance.net/resncreative) describes a creative digital agency founded in 2004, with a practice at the intersection of digital culture and experimentation. This profile is useful for positioning context but is not independent evidence of current headcount or office structure.

**Governing concept and durable value.** Resn’s durable quality is permission for a studio identity to be strange, humorous, and technologically adventurous. It is not one repeatable portal or material. The work archive and long practice history are what remain beyond the hero.

**Home, navigation, motion, rendering, and assets.** The official home presents a deliberately sparse entry vocabulary—`Discover`, `Showreel`, `Audio`, and `View all projects`. `Browser observation:` interaction and media are foregrounded before conventional business explanation on the public home. Exact renderer, framework, CMS, shaders, and asset pipeline are not documented in the primary evidence reviewed and therefore remain unknown.

**Project presentation and production.** The visible work layer supports a broad creative-digital practice, but current cases provide less openly indexed process and role detail than Hello Monday, Locomotive, or 14islands. `Inference:` Resn is more useful to House Adel as a permission structure for tonal distinctiveness than as a case-study template.

**Mobile and accessibility.** Current keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, no-WebGL, slow-network, and failed-asset behavior were not verified. Audio is visibly part of the entrance language, so an explicit non-audio path would be essential in any borrowed pattern; this statement is a House Adel requirement, not a claim about Resn’s implementation.

**House Adel assessment.** Reuse the confidence to make the studio voice authored and unexpected. Reject the idea that weirdness alone is a governing concept. House Adel’s intimate events, releases, and cultural presentations require more legible service and project proof than an effects-forward entrance provides.

## Dogstudio

**Classification:** future-scale multidisciplinary reference.

**Business layer.** [Dogstudio’s studio page](https://dogstudio.co/studio/) presents a multidisciplinary practice spanning brand strategy, branding and art direction, digital products, emerging technology, interactive installations, visual design, animation, and illustration. Its [values](https://dogstudio.co/values/) reinforce a deliberately irreverent internal culture. The [careers page](https://dogstudio.co/careers/) used the phrase “32 freaks & geeks” on the access date, providing a directional scale signal rather than a guaranteed current payroll count. The studio serves brand, technology, entertainment, arts, and culture clients.

**Governing concept and durable value.** The studio’s persistent layer is an unapologetic personality plus the ability to combine art, design, and technology. Client worlds change; the house voice remains candid and playful. That separation is relevant to House Adel, even though the tone itself is not.

**Home, navigation, motion, rendering, and assets.** The [official home](https://dogstudio.co/) presents featured projects and descriptive case destinations. Its capability mix supplies evidence for a substantial multidisciplinary production model. The official [AI practice page](https://ai.dogstudio.co/) describes generative-AI client work and frames the company as a global creative innovation and design studio. Exact current portfolio framework, renderer, and asset pipeline are not documented here. No claim is made that the home uses a particular WebGL library.

**Project presentation and production.** Dogstudio is useful because art direction and technical production sit inside one offer. The services imply access to people who can produce identity, illustration, animation, digital products, installations, and emerging-technology prototypes. That is a materially different operating condition from a solo studio assembling five asset-heavy worlds.

**Mobile and accessibility.** Current semantic order, keyboard behavior, reduced-motion treatment, no-WebGL fallback, and slow-network behavior remain unverified in this pass.

**House Adel assessment.** Reuse the distinction between a recognizable studio voice and heterogeneous client work. Do not borrow the gaming-adjacent swagger, irreverent copy, or technology-as-personality. A House Adel system should feel authored, intimate, and editorial rather than loud by default.

## Immersive Garden

**Classification:** future-scale luxury and cultural production reference.

**Business layer.** [Immersive Garden](https://immersive-g.com/) describes itself as a Paris-based global digital design and strategy studio working with ambitious brands. Its [project index](https://immersive-g.com/projects) includes luxury, culture, hospitality, and fashion work and exposes categories such as design, technology, strategy, 3D, experience, ecommerce, film, and NFT. Examples visible on the access date included Louis Vuitton VIA, David Whyte, Cartier, Chartogne-Taillet, and Dioriviera.

**Governing concept and durable value.** The studio’s consistent layer is cinematic polish and luxury-grade orchestration, while the project subject and art direction vary. After the hero, the work breadth and disciplinary labels make the practice legible.

**Home, navigation, motion, rendering, and assets.** The project index is the essential durable route. `Browser observation:` high-finish image, film, typography, and motion are central to the public presentation. The source does not name the current renderer, framework, CMS, shader system, or asset transforms; all remain unknown.

**Project presentation and production.** The visible mix of film, 3D, design, strategy, and ecommerce is evidence that the studio’s experiences are supported by multiple production disciplines. `Inference:` recreating the surface quality with a compact team would require external art, film, 3D, and optimization partners or a sharply reduced number of worlds. This is explicitly an inference from the documented capability breadth, not a headcount claim.

**Mobile and accessibility.** Current mobile route parity, keyboard behavior, reduced-motion, audio alternatives, semantic link quality, and failure states were not verified.

**House Adel assessment.** This is a relevant future benchmark for intimate hospitality, cultural, artist, and brand-launch work. The reusable lesson is the integration of art direction and production, not a default luxury-cinematic surface. House Adel should study how each client’s subject earns its own material and pacing while retaining a direct project index.

## Lusion

**Classification:** future-scale technical production reference.

**Business layer.** [Lusion’s about page](https://lusion.co/about/) calls the company a creative production studio and describes a worldwide specialist network across design, motion, 3D, and technology, led by founder and creative director Edan Kwan. Its stated areas include strategy, creative, WebGL development, frontend development, Unity/Unreal, installations, AR/VR, procedural modeling, 3D asset creation and optimization, animation, and 3D pipelines. A preserved [earlier about page](https://v2.lusion.co/about/) states that the studio was founded in 2017 with a real-time-development focus.

**Governing concept and durable value.** Lusion’s persistent identity is production expertise in real-time and spatial media. It does not need one visual skin because capability and craft are the house-level proposition.

**Home, navigation, motion, rendering, and assets.** The public portfolio showcases interactive work, but the sources reviewed do not document the renderer of the current home. WebGL, Unity, Unreal, and 3D optimization are verified service capabilities; they must not be generalized to every route. The most valuable evidence is the explicit asset pipeline: creation, compositing, optimization, animation, and runtime development are named parts of the work.

**Project presentation and production.** The service description makes visible what many effects-led portfolios hide: real-time visuals require modeling, materials, animation, optimization, and technical delivery. That production model is a useful benchmark for House Adel’s asset-world planning.

**Mobile and accessibility.** Current keyboard navigation, mobile feature reductions, reduced motion, no-WebGL fallback, and slow-network states are not documented in the reviewed primary sources.

**House Adel assessment.** Treat Lusion as a capacity reality check. If a proposed world needs the capabilities listed above, House Adel must name the collaborator, master format, optimization path, mobile substitute, fallback, and update owner. The studio should not imply Lusion-level real-time production through generic shader effects.

## MakeMePulse

**Classification:** future-scale creative-technology and luxury reference.

**Business layer.** [MakeMePulse’s “Who we are” page](https://www.makemepulse.com/who-we-are/) describes a global creative and technology agency founded in 2008, working across Paris, London, and the United States. It names strategy, design, and technology and references luxury, culture, beauty, and technology clients. The page states 180 awarded projects and a 90% returning-client rate; these are self-reported company figures. Its company-maintained [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/makemepulse) lists an 11–50-person range.

**Governing concept and durable value.** The house proposition is the combination of concept, design, and proprietary technical production. It is a studio capability system rather than one repeatable narrative metaphor.

**Home, navigation, motion, rendering, and assets.** The studio officially names NanoGL as its proprietary real-time 3D engine. That is strong evidence of internal infrastructure. It does not prove that the current portfolio home uses NanoGL, nor does it reveal the current CMS or asset-delivery system.

**Project presentation and production.** The duration of the practice, multi-market presence, proprietary engine, and luxury/culture client mix make this a future-scale benchmark. The useful business lesson is that technical differentiation is connected to repeat client work and delivery capability, not shown as an isolated experiment.

**Mobile and accessibility.** The reviewed sources do not document current mobile tiers, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, no-WebGL behavior, or semantic fallbacks.

**House Adel assessment.** Reuse the integration of business promise and technical capability. Do not treat a custom engine as a relevant near-term goal. House Adel’s differentiation should come first from choosing and shaping compact experiences well; tools can become proprietary only when repeated work proves the need.

## North Kingdom

**Classification:** future-scale gamified-experience reference.

**Business layer.** [North Kingdom’s about page](https://www.northkingdom.com/about) describes a practice founded in 2003 that combines storytelling, design, and technology for gamified experiences. It lists web and graphic design, 3D, full-stack development, AI, VR/AR, and prototyping. Its [work index](https://www.northkingdom.com/work) shows entertainment, brand, and game-publisher work, while the [careers page](https://www.northkingdom.com/careers) indicates an organization beyond a compact auteur practice.

**Governing concept and durable value.** The studio’s durable competency is turning stories into participatory systems. Interaction is treated as rules, progression, and feedback rather than a decorative transition.

**Home, navigation, motion, rendering, and assets.** The official capability list supports claims about 3D, full-stack, AI, VR/AR, and prototyping. Exact current portfolio frameworks and renderers are not documented in the evidence reviewed. `Inference:` the public positioning is game-adjacent because “gamified experiences” is a named specialty and the work includes game publishers; this does not mean every project uses game conventions.

**Project presentation and production.** The studio is useful for thinking about interaction models with meaningful state and progression. It is less useful as a direct visual or tonal benchmark for an intimate creative studio.

**Mobile and accessibility.** Current keyboard support, touch alternatives, reduced motion, no-WebGL fallback, and semantic route parity were not verified.

**House Adel assessment.** Reuse the discipline of defining interaction rules and feedback. Reject game-menu language, achievement structures, portal selection, and “world unlock” conventions unless a specific client occasion requires them. The multiverse must describe relationships among projects, not turn the portfolio into a game.

## Group conclusion

These studios demonstrate that high-production interactive work rests on specialist roles, asset pipelines, internal tools, and repeated delivery. They should raise House Adel’s standard for production evidence while lowering the temptation to imitate the visible result. The most transferable rule is simple: technical architecture should be the consequence of an experience House Adel is equipped to author and maintain.
