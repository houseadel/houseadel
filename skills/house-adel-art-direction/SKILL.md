# House Adel Art Direction

## Check authority before directing

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/decision-log.md`, approved briefs, and relevant research.
2. Label the governing concept, nexus model, and asset direction as `Approved`, `Pending`, or `Hypothesis`.
3. Preserve approved decisions. If approval is absent, develop reviewable options or isolated tests; do not quietly establish a final portfolio direction.
4. Preserve the name **House Adel**. Treat legacy “House of Adel” material as historical evidence only.

## Separate the authored system from project worlds

Define two layers for every proposal:

- **House Adel layer:** the approved governing idea, editorial voice, information hierarchy, navigation contracts, accessibility, pacing principles, and recurring signals of authorship.
- **Project-world layer:** the client's subject, palette, typography, imagery, material language, sound, motion, and interaction metaphors.

Allow project worlds to change radically without turning the portfolio into unrelated themes. Do not let a client's aesthetic redefine the studio shell, and do not force House Adel styling onto client work.

Treat a multiverse, if retained, as a meaningful relationship between realities rather than a theme picker. Treat shattered fragments and portals as hypotheses until approved.

## Direct assets before effects

Start each proposed world with:

1. a one-sentence idea and world brief;
2. a visual bible covering typography, imagery, material, light, composition, color, motion, sound, and interaction;
3. a reference pack annotated with principles to learn and details not to copy;
4. one master frame strong enough to judge without animation;
5. a provenance record and production plan;
6. desktop, tablet, mobile, reduced-motion, static, slow-network, and failed-asset treatments.

Do not use CSS effects, WebGL, or motion to conceal weak imagery, typography, or project material.

## Challenge every proposal

Require clear answers to these questions:

- What idea does the choice communicate, and why is it specific to this project?
- Which approved House Adel rule does it preserve?
- Which world-specific rule does it express?
- Does it still work after the first 30 seconds and without its headline effect?
- Does the typography remain readable and intentional?
- Can the asset quality, rights, mobile reinterpretation, accessibility, and production burden be sustained?
- What comes from a reference, and how has the principle been transformed rather than copied?

Reject generic AI imagery, Awwwards pastiche, Marvel multiverse language, gaming menus, stock portal motifs, indiscriminate particles, glowing-button UI, decorative custom cursors, and five skins sharing one layout. Reject novelty that has no narrative or functional role.

## Record and present decisions

For each consequential visual choice, record the decision, status, rationale, evidence, rejected alternatives, affected worlds and variants, asset dependencies, accessibility or performance cost, and approver. Add approved material decisions to `docs/decision-log.md`.

Present a direction review with the visual result, intended meaning, evidence, principal tradeoff, production implications, and explicit decision requested. Keep unresolved risks visible and do not substitute terminal descriptions for reviewable visual evidence.
