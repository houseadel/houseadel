# Positioning

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Internal. Nothing in this file is public copy, and two sections of it must never become public copy: the service levels and their prices, and the reference studios. `docs/COPY_APPROVED.md` is what the site actually says; this file is the reasoning that decides what may be said there at all.

## The short version

House Adel makes art-directed front-end websites. The impact comes from layout, typography, motion, interaction, image treatment, sequencing, scroll behaviour, transitions and creative direction. That is a narrower claim than "immersive studio" and a more defensible one: it is what the studio can deliver today, at a standard worth paying for.

Practices, studios and brands come first. Singular occasions come second, in a trailing clause, and MARVELL 20 sits comfortably under both.

## Who the work is for

Businesses whose clients judge them partly on taste, and whose existing content is decent but presented without intention:

- law and accountancy practices
- consultants and recruitment companies
- architecture studios and interior designers
- photographers and florists
- boutique hotels and luxury service businesses

The offer to all of them is the same: take the content that already exists and make it feel considerably more deliberate.

Occasions — weddings, anniversaries, dinners, launches — remain real work and remain welcome. They are named second because leading with them made the studio look like an invitation supplier, and because a law firm reading an invitation studio's website does not recognise itself.

## The three service levels

Internal pricing. These never appear on the site, in the enquiry form, or in any public writing, and `docs/VOICE.md`'s standing ban on price, budget and investment copy is not lifted by this file. They exist so quoting is consistent and so scope stays inside what the studio can actually build.

### 1. Art-Directed Website — US$3,000 to 6,000

The bulk of the business, and it should stay the bulk of the business.

React or plain HTML, a CMS, GSAP, forms. Possibly no WebGL at all. The value is entirely in composition, type, pacing and image treatment. This is legitimate high-value work and should never be described apologetically.

### 2. Interactive Website — US$5,000 to 10,000

The same architecture, plus one or two signature interactions. Examples of the right size:

- a hero image that refracts subtly under the cursor
- architectural projects that unfold spatially on scroll
- a gallery that bends into a helix
- images that transition through a custom mask
- a page laid out as an editorial spread rather than stacked rectangles

One technically memorable thing, not twenty. The discipline is in the count.

### 3. Experimental Front End — US$8,000 and up

Eventually, and only when the project genuinely calls for it. This is not a standing offer yet. Three.js, WebGL, or a supplied 3D model, scoped tightly: one interactive object, not an entire navigable 3D hotel. That distinction is the difference between a project that ships and one that does not.

## The capability envelope

The studio borrows taste and interaction philosophy from studios working at the top of the field. It does not borrow their claims. Nothing on the site, in a proposal, or in a reply to an enquiry may present House Adel as producing:

- original 3D pipelines or in-house 3D production
- custom physical or hardware installations
- virtual-reality or headset systems
- real-time multiplayer experiences
- campaign-scale engineering or product-scale infrastructure
- custom backend infrastructure as a standard deliverable

`src/pages/TermsPage.tsx` already excludes these contractually, in both languages. This file is why.

Supplied 3D assets are fine. A single WebGL object is fine. An on-site tool that runs in a browser, as MARVELL 20's did, is fine and is described as exactly that. The line is between using a technique and claiming a pipeline.

## Reference studios

Immersive Garden and Active Theory, behavioural only and never named publicly, in line with `AGENTS.md`'s standing rule on references. `docs/DECISIONS.md` records what has already been taken from each: near-camera botanical framing from one, tonal structure in a dark viewport from the other. Both are principles, not assets.

What is borrowed is taste and interaction philosophy: judgement about restraint, pacing and interaction quality. What is not borrowed is any implication of matching technical depth. They run original 3D pipelines, installations and campaign-scale engineering teams; House Adel does not, and a studio that markets a capability it has to sub-contract or improvise gets found out on the first project that tests it.
