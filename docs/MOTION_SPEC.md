# Motion Specification

Last updated: 2026-08-10

## Purpose and grammar

Motion exists only to introduce the studio, change spatial context, explain capability, preserve continuity between pages, or give meaningful feedback. The House Adel grammar is assembly, uncovering, framing, restrained depth change, material light, and typographic masking. Native scrolling remains authoritative.

Simple hover, focus, pressed, and menu-glyph changes use CSS. GSAP is reserved for coordinated entrance, route transition, capability-state choreography, and the optional spatial opening. Every GSAP owner scopes its work and reverts on cleanup.

## Timing

- Micro feedback: 120–220ms.
- Menu state: 280–480ms.
- Capability state: 320–620ms.
- Route cover/reveal: approximately 650–900ms total on desktop, shorter on mobile.
- Loader: readiness-driven, skippable, and shorter on repeat visits. The first-visit signature sequence may exceed 1200ms under the `house-adel-motion` skill's signature-only allowance: it stays readiness-driven rather than fixed-duration, Skip resolves it immediately at any frame, it never blocks navigation, and it does not repeat on a return visit.
- Spatial scroll: desktop only and bounded to its opening section.
- Cursor companion, magnetic hover: within the 80–280ms micro-feedback bands.
- Liquid lens: the injection point is on the pointer with no easing at all; the field's own persistence is roughly 800ms to the mask threshold and fully settled inside three seconds.
- Scroll text reveal, marquee, parallax: entrance/stagger within the 160–600ms bands; marquee's own loop and parallax's scrub are continuous but bounded, not decorative drift.

No essential content waits for an animation to finish. Repeated generic fade-up choreography, cursor replacement (hiding or substituting the native cursor), scroll hijacking, and autoplay audio remain prohibited.

Two rules govern the lens. Its colour is only ever produced by moving sample points, never chosen: the trail's own silhouette is read again at four increasing offsets along the boundary normal, and the ring between each copy and the one inside it takes the next stop of the spectrum — cyan, blue-violet, magenta, warm red — so the edge is a stack of displaced outlines rather than a tinted line. The flow's angle of incidence on the boundary opens the stack along a stroke's leading edge and closes it behind, and it collapses altogether at rest. And the lens stands down entirely over a modelled subject, fringe included, because that form already answers the hand in the same spectrum by dispersing its own particles — two answers to one gesture is one too many, and the louder of them flattens the thing it covers.

That spectrum is one ramp, shared. The lens's fringe, the relief's dispersal, the pearl clouds' lift and the dissolve's spectral edge all read from the same four stops, so a hand crossing from the type to the sculpture to the wood is met by one material rather than three that happen to be colourful.

The liquid lens is not a mouse trail, and the distinction is worth stating because the two look alike in a description and nothing alike on screen. A trail is a decoration drawn *over* the page: stamps of its own colour, accumulating, with the page unaffected underneath. The lens draws nothing. It is a mask over the finished composite that says which pixels of the site invert, so what is seen inside it is House Adel itself in its other tonal state; every band of its fringe is that same mask displaced, which is refraction, not paint; and overlapping passes merge into one body rather than stacking. It also cannot sit behind anything, which is the property that made every earlier attempt fail.

## Verification contract

Each production interaction must retain: visible purpose, route ownership, trigger, reverse/re-target behaviour, resize strategy, mobile strategy, reduced-motion state, no-WebGL state where relevant, keyboard/touch equivalent, cleanup owner, measured bundle impact, and a static semantic result. Playwright covers route changes, Back/Forward, menu keyboard operation, capability selection, language, sound opt-in, reduced motion, no-WebGL, loader states, accessibility, and reviewed visual baselines.
